Q Does the cellular interface become a genuine sense/act loop when the environment is a real diffusing spatial field the cell reads and acts back on?

Claim

Compiling the Fig 5 draft installs a real Laplacian-diffusion field and single-cell uptake; the cell depletes nutrient locally, diffusion refills it, and the cell reshapes the very gradient it depends on (niche construction) — interface preserved (Law 2).

Fig 5 turns the cellular interface (Fig 4) into a relationship. The environment is not a set of scalars but a field: `environment.chemical_field` is refined from a scalar concentration into a real `map[float]` lattice — a 9-cell 1-D grid with a unit bump at the centre cell — and a real finite-difference diffusion process (`SpatialDiffusion`, `D · ∇²field · interval`, zero-flux/Neumann edges) runs on it, mass-conserving to round-off. A localized source-and-decay process (`ProductionDegradationField`) and a scalar mechanical-stress relaxation (`MechanicalRelax`) act on the shared fields alongside diffusion. The single cell (`SingleCellSpatial`) samples the chemical grid at its own lattice index and couples to it locally: it senses the field value there and acts back by taking up matter, which both feeds a biomass/shape proxy and depletes the field at that location; diffusion then carries the depletion outward from neighbouring cells. The cell also exerts traction (driven by the local chemical value plus the shared mechanical field) and drifts chemotactically up the local gradient. The key conceptual move is that sensing and acting are not two subsystems but one coupling: the same shared field store is what the cell reads and what it writes back to — the cell measurably reshapes the very gradient it depends on (niche construction), all behind the Fig 4 interface preserved unchanged (Law 2). Measured on the compiled fig05-executable: the cell draws down the bolus at its location (the field cell it senses, 1.0 → 0.2) as it takes up matter (uptake flux 0 → 0.34) and acts back mechanically (traction 0 → 0.41, mechanical field 0 → 0.94).

Test

Install a diffusing spatial nutrient field and a sense-and-act mechanism behind the Fig 5 interface, and run them over one shared field store. The compiler must preserve the declared interface exactly.

Conclusion

Claims

  • The same interface that Fig 4 specifies closes into a sense/act loop over a real diffusing field: the cell draws down the bolus it senses (a field cell 1.0→0.2, uptake flux 0→0.34) and acts back mechanically (traction 0→0.41, mechanical field 0→0.94) — sensing and acting are one coupling over the shared field store, and the cell measurably reshapes the very gradient it depends on (niche construction), all behind an interface preserved unchanged (Law 2).

Evidence

  • CELL-DRAWS-WELL — uptake flux 0.0 → 0.34; bolus cell drawn down 1.0 → 0.2
  • CELL-ACTS-BACK — traction 0.0 → 0.41
  • MECHANICAL-RESPONSE — mechanical field 0.0 → 0.94

Limitations

  • Toy-real constants: diffusivity, uptake rate, biomass yield, and traction coefficient are illustrative, not fitted to a named organism or medium.
  • A single diffusing field on a small 9-cell lattice with one cell; no multi-cell competition or cross-feeding (that is the new cell-cell-coupling study), and field mass is not tracked as a closed balance across the whole system.
  • Niche construction is shown as a pattern (the cell measurably depletes its local field value) — not validated against a quantitative ecological or eco-evolutionary claim.
  • Interface preservation (Law 2) is checked structurally; the chemical-field leaf's schema is enriched (scalar → grid) while the declared ports and wiring are unchanged.

Next steps

  • Add a second cell to the field to test competition and cross-feeding over the shared store (see the new cell-cell-coupling study).
  • Track field mass as an explicit closed balance (source + decay + diffusion + uptake) to quantify niche construction rather than showing it qualitatively.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • The environment's chemical field is a small, real 9-cell 1-D lattice (`map[float]`, GRID_N=9 in `handlers_fig05.py`), not a scalar stand-in and not a 15-node grid — corrected here from an earlier, larger figure quoted for the pre-split typed-interface study.
  • Interface preservation (Law 2) is checked structurally: the compiler auto-detects that the handlers' `chemical_field` ports wire to a refined (grid) store and relaxes conformance for the leaf's schema only; the external interface (ports + wired paths) is unchanged.

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • The coupling is a single diffusing field on a small 9-cell 1-D lattice with one cell; no multi-cell competition or cross-feeding, and field mass is not tracked as a closed balance across the whole system (diffusion alone is mass-conserving to round-off; source/decay/uptake are not reconciled against it).
  • Constants (uptake rate, biomass yield, diffusivity, traction coefficient) are illustrative toy-real values, not fitted to a named organism or medium.
  • Niche construction is shown as a pattern — the cell measurably depletes the field value it senses — not as a validated quantitative ecological result.
  • Interface preservation (Law 2) is structural: the compiler checks port names, types, and wiring; the chemical-field leaf's schema is enriched from scalar to grid because the handler environment explicitly refines it, and that refinement is itself part of the declared compilation contract, not a silent change.
