Q When a single cell metabolizes at its own location, does it measurably reshape the very nutrient gradient it depends on — environmental modification (local resource depletion + secretion) — closing the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 4) as real spatial metabolism: one cell sensing local glucose, running flux-balance analysis at its own footprint, growing, and secreting a byproduct that diffuses back into the field it senses? The paper frames this feedback loop as "the minimal structure required for control"; here the loop is genuinely closed (a real depletion → uptake feedback) in the flagship, while directed control — chemotaxis up the sensed gradient — is the flagship's non-motile-by-design other half, now BUILT and MEASURED in the `chemotaxis` variant (finding F-08). Mechanistically, the cell and its environment are two real, independently-built simulators (viva-cpm's Cellular Potts engine, spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes) composed through one typed coupling process (`CpmCellField`), rather than a scripted or lumped stand-in for that coupling.

Claim

Composing a real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed coupling process (`CpmCellField`) reproduces the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 4) as genuine spatial metabolism, not a scripted stand-in for it (the flagship loop is closed; directed control/chemotaxis is built and measured in the `chemotaxis` variant, finding F-08) — over 20 ticks the cell runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint (`e_coli_core`, O2-capped to force acetate overflow), grows ~3.4x in CPM volume (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of a 60x60 lattice it does not come close to filling), depletes local glucose by up to ~18% peak-to-trough (~6% from the initial condition) before diffusion partially resupplies it (environmental modification), and secretes a diffusing acetate plume — two independently-built simulators talking through one typed port.

A cell does not merely inhabit its environment — it remakes it. Here one cell sits in a nutrient gradient, eats at its own location, and secretes a byproduct back into the field, measurably reshaping the very gradient it depends on: environmental modification (local resource depletion + secretion), realized spatially. This is the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 4), and this investigation's flagship. The paper frames this feedback loop as "the minimal structure required for control": here the loop is genuinely closed (a real depletion → uptake feedback), while directed control — chemotaxis up the sensed gradient — is the flagship's non-motile-by-design other half, now built and measured in the `chemotaxis` variant (finding F-08); the FLAGSHIP cell itself senses, metabolizes, grows, and secretes, but does not act on what it senses. Mechanically it composes two real, independently-built simulators through one typed coupling process (`CpmCellField`): one CPM cell — a real Cellular Potts Model agent from viva-cpm, with genuine pixel-level shape, adhesion, and volume dynamics on a 60x60 lattice — with a shared spatio-flux `fields` grid (glucose, acetate) running `DiffusionAdvection`. `CpmCellField` is the entire interface between them: each tick it reads the mean glucose concentration at the cell's current footprint (the set of lattice pixels the CPM cell occupies), solves one dynamic-FBA step against `e_coli_core` with Michaelis–Menten-limited glucose uptake (`glucose_km`, `glucose_vmax`) and a fixed oxygen cap (`oxygen_vmax`) — the O2 cap is what forces the optimizer into mixed-acid acetate overflow rather than pure aerobic respiration, matching the biological mechanism this coupling is meant to demonstrate. That causal claim is not left to the code: the O2-uncapped mechanism control (`o2-cap-forces-acetate-overflow` below) lifts the oxygen bound and shows acetate secretion collapses to ~0 while the capped flagship secretes a real plume, so the overflow is a demonstrated experiment rather than an assertion. The coupling then mass-balances the FBA solution against how much glucose the footprint actually holds (so the cell can never manufacture biomass or acetate from glucose it did not actually remove), grows the CPM cell's target volume in proportion to the resulting biomass, and writes the clamped glucose uptake and acetate secretion back into the field as a spatial delta. spatio-flux's own `DiffusionAdvection` then spreads both species outward: the depleted local glucose is partially resupplied from the field's pre-set left-low-to-right-high gradient, and the secreted acetate forms an expanding plume. Measured on `single-cell-in-a-field` over 20 ticks (`tests/test_flagship_field.py`, `tests/test_cpm_cell_field.py`, `tests/test_cpm_viz.py`): the CPM cell grows from 32 to 110 lattice pixels (about 3.4x), against a 60x60 = 3600-pixel lattice — roughly 3% coverage at the end, nowhere near filling the field. Local footprint glucose peaks at 1.15 (t=2, after diffusion briefly outpaces the cell's own uptake) then falls to 0.94 by t=20 — ~18% peak-to-trough (the peak at t=2 is created by diffusion inflow after t=0) and ~6% from the initial condition — as the cell's own consumption outpaces the partial resupply diffusion provides. Field-wide, net glucose falls by about 32 units (5940 -> 5908, out of the field's full 3600-pixel total mass) — a small fraction of the whole field, because only the cell's own footprint and its immediate diffusive neighborhood are touched, which is exactly the local, footprint-scale environmental modification the composite is built to show rather than a field-wide depletion event. Acetate, starting at zero everywhere, rises to a field-wide total of about 47 by t=20 as the secreted byproduct diffuses into a spreading plume. Biomass (the FBA-tracked internal state driving CPM growth) rises from 0.1 to about 0.37.

Test

Run `single-cell-in-a-field.composite.json` — a real CPM cell (viva-cpm) coupled via `CpmCellField` to a real spatio-flux glucose/acetate field running `DiffusionAdvection` — to completion over 20 ticks through the process-bigraph engine, and measure whether the cell metabolizes (dynamic-FBA against `e_coli_core`, O2-capped to force acetate overflow), grows (both biomass and CPM lattice volume), depletes the local nutrient it senses (environmental modification), and secretes a diffusing acetate plume.

The `single-cell-in-a-field-steadystate` variant adds a first-order glucose source, a first-order acetate decay sink, and a logistic biomass carrying capacity, and is run ~120 ticks to a VERIFIED flux steady state — d(biomass)/dt and both field fluxes fall below tolerance while the cell keeps metabolizing at maintenance. This answers the limitation the flagship itself flags (the field has no continuous source or decay term).·M7d cadence rigor check (peer review) — the flagship's operator-split CPM<->dFBA<->diffusion loop runs at coupling cadence 1, and only the PDE substeps are CFL-guarded today. Halving then quartering the coupling cadence (sub-step interval 1 -> 0.5 -> 0.25) and measuring the drift in the four gated observables between the two finest levels tests whether the pinned trajectories are cadence-converged. Measured head-to-head over 20 ticks — up-gradient displacement (chemo vs control), sensed local glucose, net glucose acquired, biomass, and CPM volume.·Byte-identical no-op control — chemotaxis_strength 0.0 (the flagship default) leaves the flagship trajectory unchanged. With the force off, CpmCellField never calls set_external_potential, so the world's external potential stays all-zero and a run with the flag EXPLICITLY 0.0 is identical, to the bit, to a run with the flag ABSENT. This proves the chemotaxis term is a pure add-on that does not perturb the existing flagship.

Result

Over 20 ticks the cell grows ~3.4x in CPM volume (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of a 60x60 lattice it does not fill), depletes its own local glucose by ~18% peak-to-trough (~6% from the initial condition) before diffusion partially resupplies it, and secretes a diffusing acetate plume (field-wide acetate 0 -> ~47.0) from an O2-capped dynamic-FBA overflow mechanism — demonstrated to be O2-cap-caused by an uncapped control (uncapped acetate ~0). The paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 4) closed as genuine spatial metabolism (directed control/chemotaxis built and measured in the `chemotaxis` variant, F-08), composed from two independently-built simulators through one typed process.

◐ INTERFACE SURROGATE CALIBRATION — a tuned Michaelis-Menten twin reproduces the flagship's cell↔field interface observables, this investigation's test of the paper's substitutability idea at the level of surrogate calibration. The paper (§2 / §Cellular interface) argues that "this substitutability is not only a software convenience. It allows the interface itself to become the object of comparison: different mechanisms may realize the same externally observable relation", and that "different internal organizations may sometimes be represented through the same coarse-grained interface when they preserve the interaction variables relevant to that description." Here that idea is put to a first test: the flagship cell's INTERNAL metabolism is swapped from constraint-based dynamic-FBA (cobra e_coli_core, O2-capped) to a lumped, cobra-free Michaelis–Menten / Monod metabolism (`single-cell-in-a-field-mm`, `mechanism: mm`) behind the BYTE-IDENTICAL `CpmCellField` ports, and the interface-level observables are compared head-to-head. Confirmed: over 20 ticks the tuned MM twin reproduces the coarse-grained cell↔field interface — final biomass dFBA 0.370 vs MM 0.342 (7.5%), CPM volume 110 vs 99 px (10%), total acetate secreted 47.02 vs 47.02 (~0%), net glucose depleted 31.6 vs 33.6 units (6.2%); every interface observable agrees within ~10%. The MM twin additionally runs with NO cobra at all — the surrogate-model payoff, a mechanism that runs where the original could not. This is surrogate calibration, not yet mechanism-independence: the 4 MM params (mm_vmax 4.0, mm_km 0.5, mm_yield 0.024, mm_overflow 1.4) were TUNED to preserve the interaction variables (4 knobs fit to 4 observables — exactly determined) — the claim is "a tuned surrogate matches the coarse-grained relation", not identical internals, and not a derivation of MM from the dFBA solution. HELD-OUT TEST NOW RUN (peer-review M2) — the named next step is done, and re-levels this finding honestly on both sides. With the 4 MM params FROZEN at their tuned values, dFBA vs MM were compared on conditions neither was tuned on (tests/test_substitutability_heldout.py). Inside the calibrated glucose-limited, fixed-O2 regime the substitution is MECHANISM-INDEPENDENT, not just a fit — a held-out initial glucose field (flat 3.0, not the tuned 0.3→3.0 gradient) agrees within 11.9% on every observable, and a 2x-longer run (40 ticks) within 12.5%. But the lumped MM box carries NO oxygen variable, and that is its blind spot — on a held-out O2 cap (dFBA 2.5→5.0) dFBA nearly quadruples its biomass/volume while the O2-blind MM twin is unchanged, so agreement COLLAPSES to ~74%. The honest re-level — mechanism-independence WITHIN the calibrated regime (held-out within ~13% on the glucose/time axes), surrogate calibration only ACROSS the O2 axis the MM box has no variable for. Degrees of freedom — 4 free MM params vs 4 interface observables (exactly determined), so the tuned fit was guaranteed a priori; the in-regime held-out agreement is the load-bearing evidence, and the O2-axis collapse is the load-bearing limitation. via interface-substitutable-held-out

✓ STEADY-STATE REGIME (M7d, peer review) — the flagship is a pure transient (no source terms), so every flagship number is a transient reading, never a source/sink equilibrium, and the study itself flags this as a limitation. Adding three cell-independent terms behind the same `CpmCellField` ports — a first-order glucose source (grid-wide relaxation toward a setpoint), a first-order acetate decay sink, and a logistic biomass carrying capacity — turns the composite into a chemostat-like regime that reaches a VERIFIED flux steady state. Confirmed — over the final 10 of 120 ticks the per-tick relative change of biomass (0.0015%), field glucose (0.0001%), and field acetate (0.014%) all fall below the 0.1% steady-state tolerance, with standing values biomass ~0.500, CPM volume ~147 px, local glucose ~1.356, field glucose ~5372 (uptake balanced by source), field acetate ~31.5 (secretion balanced by decay); the cell is alive and metabolizing at the fixed point. Note — a biomass dilution term alone gives NO interior fixed point in this coupling (per-pixel uptake scales with biomass/footprint-area, so local glucose equilibrates biomass-independently and mu never falls to a dilution rate); the logistic carrying capacity is what forces every flux to a steady state. This demonstrates the source/sink-equilibrium regime the flagship deferred. via steadystate-flux-equilibrium

✓ CHEMOTAXIS — DIRECTED UP-GRADIENT MOTION, BUILT AND MEASURED. The flagship senses, metabolizes, grows, and secretes but does NOT act on what it senses — directed control (chemotaxis up the sensed gradient) was the deferred half of the paper's sense → metabolize → act loop. The `chemotaxis` variant closes it behind the same `CpmCellField` ports — each tick the coupling process takes grad(glucose) of the SHARED spatio-flux field over the cell's footprint and sets viva-cpm's per-type external-potential force f = chemotaxis_strength·grad(glucose), so the Metropolis pixel-copy is biased up-gradient via U(r) = −f·r (the linearized form of CC3D chemotaxis ΔH = −λ·Δc, driven by the external shared field the cell already senses, not a viva-cpm-internal PDE field — which has no write path in this coupling). RESULT — directed up-gradient motion is genuinely achieved and it measurably helps. Over 20 ticks on the identical field the chemotaxis cell migrates from COM x ≈ 18.5 to ≈ 39.3 — a +20.8 px up-gradient displacement versus the non-motile flagship control's +0.2 px thermal drift (~90×) — reaching a ~2.1× richer local glucose region (sensed local glucose 1.95 vs 0.94) and, for it, acquiring ~16% more glucose (net 36.8 vs 31.6), building ~13% more biomass (0.418 vs 0.370), and growing ~36% more CPM volume (150 vs 110 px). The term is off by default (chemotaxis_strength 0.0 never sets the force → flagship byte-identical). Confirmed — the "chemotaxis is deferred" limitation the flagship flagged is now built and measured, and the honest result is positive on both axes (directed motion AND a nutrient/growth advantage). via chemotaxis-moves-cell-up-gradient-and-helps

Conclusion

Claims

  • A single cell reshapes the very nutrient gradient it depends on — environmental modification (local resource depletion + secretion), realized spatially. Composing a real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField) reproduces the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 4) as genuine spatial metabolism — the loop closed (depletion → uptake feedback), with directed control (chemotaxis) the flagship's non-motile-by-design other half, now built and measured in the chemotaxis variant (finding F-08 — directed up-gradient migration with a nutrient/growth advantage). Over 20 ticks the cell grows ~3.4x in CPM volume (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of a 60x60 lattice it does not come close to filling), depletes its own local glucose by ~18% peak-to-trough (~6% from the initial condition) before diffusion partially resupplies it (environmental modification), and secretes a diffusing acetate plume from an O2-capped dynamic-FBA overflow mechanism.

Evidence

  • CELL-METABOLIZES-AND-GROWS — biomass 0.1 -> ~0.37 via dynamic-FBA at the cell's own footprint
  • CPM-VOLUME-GROWS — CPM lattice volume 32 -> 110 px (~3.4x), ~3% lattice coverage
  • ACETATE-SECRETED-AND-DIFFUSES — field-wide acetate 0 -> ~47.0 as the plume spreads
  • NET-GLUCOSE-CONSUMED — field-wide glucose 5940.0 -> ~5908.4; local footprint drawdown ~18% peak-to-trough (~6% from initial)

  • FIELD-MASS-BALANCE-CLOSES (foundational control) — glucose and acetate ledgers close to < 1e-6 (cell nets a glucose sink + acetate source; diffusion nets ~0)

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • e_coli_core constants (`glucose_km` 0.5, `glucose_vmax` 3.5, `oxygen_vmax` 2.5, `box_volume_L` 0.3, `grow_per_biomass` 300.0) are toy-real: plausible, hand-tuned magnitudes chosen to produce gradual, spatially-legible dynamics over a short (~20-25 tick) run, not fitted to a named organism, medium, or literature target.
  • The metabolized/diffused nutrient field is exclusively the spatio-flux `fields` store (glucose, acetate) that `CpmCellField` and `DiffusionAdvection` share. The CPM cell's OWN internal lattice/world (its shape, occupied pixels, footprint) lives inside viva-cpm's Rust engine as a private object the coupling process owns (`CpmCellField.world`) — it is not a process-bigraph store, and no other process in the composite reads or writes it. viva-cpm's own internal field concept (`field_at_cell`, `secretory_types`, `receptor_coupling`) is separate machinery this composite does not use.
  • Growth is roughly exponential in this coupling (biomass compounds each tick), so the run is deliberately bounded to ~20-25 ticks — a longer run would need either a saturating growth term or a much larger field to stay spatially legible rather than filling the lattice.
