Q The thesis of the investigation — one typed interface, many mechanisms — is proven at the level of a single figure (Fig 6). Does it also hold at the level of a WHOLE CELL? This capstone assembles a composed whole cell in the figures' style — cell–environment uptake, a viability-gated Fig 6 metabolism, a viability monitor, and the division and disintegration rewrites, wired through shared cell and environment stores — and then SWAPS the Fig 6 metabolism behind identical ports (coarse, saturating kinetic, or real COBRApy FBA). Same cell, three mechanisms: does each produce its own life history while the interface never moves, and does each cell still grow, divide (mass-conserved), and die?

Claim

The investigation's thesis holds at the whole-cell level: behind ONE unchanged metabolism interface, the Fig 6 mechanism is swappable (coarse / saturating-kinetic / real COBRApy FBA) and the SAME assembled cell lives three distinct life histories — peak biomass 5.78 / 6.43 / 6.05, dividing at t≈3.0 / 2.1 / 2.7. In every case the cell grows, divides once (cell_count 1 → 2, mass-conserved — the mother is halved), and dies when a scripted thermal shock (37 → 50 °C at t=12) collapses viability to 0.018 and disintegration converts biomass to debris. The cell is assembled by hand in the figures' style, not compiled from the figure drafts.

This is the payoff, and it is the paper's thesis carried up to the whole cell. The cell is ASSEMBLED BY HAND in the figures' style (meta_modelers_guide/wholecell.py) — it is NOT emitted by the compiler from the figure drafts. What it demonstrates is mechanism-swapping (Fig 6, law 4) at the WHOLE-CELL level: behind one unchanged metabolism interface (nutrients ⇒ biomass), the Fig 6 mechanism is selectable — ``build_whole_cell(metabolism="coarse"|"kinetic"|"fba")`` — and the same cell lives three DISTINCT life histories. With the COARSE lumped-yield metabolism the cell peaks at biomass 5.78 and divides at t≈3.0; with the SATURATING-KINETIC (Michaelis–Menten) metabolism it peaks at 6.43 and divides earlier, t≈2.1; with the REAL COBRApy FBA metabolism (flux-balance over e_coli_core solved each step) it peaks at 6.05 and divides at t≈2.7. Three mechanisms, three trajectories, one interface that does not move. In every case the cell first GROWS on the environmental nutrient as viability-gated metabolism converts uptake to biomass; once biomass crosses the division threshold the rewrite fires ONCE, partitioning the mother's biomass — division CONSERVES MASS (the mother is halved, not doubled) — so cell_count goes 1 → 2. Then a SCRIPTED thermal shock (the ThermalEnvironment process drives temperature 37 → 50 °C at a hard-coded t=12) pushes the cell out of its tolerance band; the viability monitor reads the interface and collapses viability 1.0 → 0.018, which gates metabolism off and triggers the disintegration rewrite — the maintained boundary fails and biomass decays into molecular debris (debris accumulating to ≈5.5–6.1, depending on the metabolism). Grow, divide once, die — but the "grow" and "divide" are mechanism-driven and mechanism-dependent, while the "die" is a scripted shock. Backing this whole-cell payoff, every one of the paper's figures compiles and runs on its own — that gallery is documented in the companion gallery study.

Conclusion

Claims

  • The thesis holds at the whole-cell level. The cell is ASSEMBLED BY HAND in the figures' style (not compiled from the figure drafts), and behind ONE unchanged metabolism interface the Fig 6 mechanism is swappable: coarse (peak biomass 5.78, divides t≈3.0), saturating-kinetic (6.43, t≈2.1), and real COBRApy FBA (6.05, t≈2.7) — same cell, three mechanisms, three life histories. In every case the cell grows, divides once (cell_count 1 → 2, mass-conserved — the mother is halved), and dies when a SCRIPTED thermal shock (37 → 50 °C hard-coded at t=12) collapses viability to 0.018 and disintegration converts biomass to molecular debris (≈5.5–6.1).

Evidence

  • METABOLISM-SWAP-COARSE — coarse metabolism: peak biomass 5.78, divides t≈3.0
  • METABOLISM-SWAP-KINETIC — saturating-kinetic metabolism: peak biomass 6.43, divides t≈2.1 (distinct)
  • METABOLISM-SWAP-FBA — real COBRApy FBA metabolism: peak biomass 6.05, divides t≈2.7
  • CELL-DIVIDES — the rewrite fires once: cell_count 1 → 2, mother halved (mass conserved)
  • CELL-DIES — scripted shock (37 → 50 °C at t=12) collapses viability 1.0 → 0.018; disintegration turns biomass to debris (≈5.5–6.1)

Limitations

  • The whole cell is ASSEMBLED BY HAND in the figures' style (wholecell.py), not emitted by the compiler from the figure drafts; it demonstrates that a Fig-6-style mechanism-swap holds at the whole-cell scale, not a calibrated whole-cell model.
  • The "grow" and "divide" behaviors are mechanism-driven and mechanism-dependent, but the "die" is a SCRIPTED thermal shock (hard-coded t=12), not an emergent failure. One deterministic run per metabolism.

Next steps

  • Replace the scripted shock with an emergent viability failure (e.g. nutrient exhaustion), run replicate seeds and thermal schedules, and drive the whole cell toward calibration rather than illustration.
