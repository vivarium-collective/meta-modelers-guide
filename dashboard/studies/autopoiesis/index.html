Q The paper's §Self-organized processes section states the autopoiesis criterion as a boundary problem, not a bookkeeping one: "A membrane alone is insufficient: a vesicle may form a boundary without constituting a living system. A stronger criterion is that the processes inside the boundary collectively contribute to maintaining the organization that, in turn, keeps those processes possible." This study puts that criterion on a 2D lattice and asks the discriminating question directly: does a membrane PERSIST because an internal production loop -- gated on the boundary itself staying topologically closed -- replenishes the membrane against decay, and does a mere vesicle with no such loop, from the identical seed, DISSIPATE? The two composites differ in exactly one variable (the production rate constant k_prod), so the contrast between persistence and collapse is the finding. beer2023's viability bounds -- constraints on a shared state that must be maintained for the composition to persist -- are the frame: closure here is that the membrane's own maintenance is conditioned on the boundary it maintains, so break the boundary and the maintenance cannot fire.

Claim

The paper's §Self-organized processes autopoiesis criterion -- a membrane persists because the processes inside the boundary maintain the organization that keeps those processes possible -- is IMPLEMENTED here as an AUTHORED operational-closure criterion and its consequences are tested. The closure-dependence is hand-coded (production is gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer), NOT emergent from local physics; the persistence, knockout, and puncture results all follow from that construction, and are worthwhile because the criterion is implemented and its consequences are verified -- not because closure arose on its own. A scalar membrane field under reaction-diffusion, with production gated on the boundary staying topologically closed and self-limited by enclosed area, PERSISTS: enclosed interior area plateaus at ~149 px (homeostasis, down from the ~465 px seed) with persists == 1.0 and no collapse within a ~1800-step window. The single-variable negative control (k_prod=0, the production loop knocked out, identical seed and wiring) COLLAPSES: closure is lost by step ~96, enclosed_area -> 0, persists == 0.0. Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation, so within this authored construction the production loop is what maintains the boundary -- confirmed independently by a fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023): breaking the boundary so nothing is enclosed shuts off the closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild from nothing. The boundaries of the claim are stated as findings: the homeostatic plateau is metastable, not eternal (the physically-correct signature of a closed system with no external influx, not a defect); the reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG, so persistence is deterministic and NOT seed-dependent (the multi-seed range is a degenerate single point); the maintenance gate is an authored global topological test, so a mechanistically-local version (an interior precursor that collapses when the membrane leaks) is the named next step, not something this build shows; and this is a minimal self-maintaining boundary demonstrating authored operational closure, NOT real membrane chemistry or metabolism.

The paper's §Self-organized processes section (its autopoiesis discussion, Fig 9) draws a sharp line a coarser, specification-stage lumped realization could only gesture at: a boundary is not enough. A vesicle forms a boundary and is still not alive; what the criterion asks for is that the processes inside the boundary maintain the very organization that keeps those processes possible. This study realizes that line spatially as a single scalar membrane-density field phi on a 64x64 periodic grid, seeded as a deterministic Gaussian-annulus ring -- a closed membrane -- evolving under a reaction-diffusion update dphi/dt = D*lap(phi) - k_decay*phi + production. The production term is the closure loop made literal: it fires ONLY where the membrane still topologically encloses an interior (binary_fill_holes(phi>thr) & ~(phi>thr)), is deposited onto existing membrane, and is throttled by the enclosed area relative to the seed's own enclosed area, so the loop self-limits into a homeostatic plateau rather than growing without bound. The membrane's ability to make boundary material is conditioned on the boundary it maintains -- exactly beer2023's viability-bound reading of closure, where a shared state must be held for the composition to persist. The discriminator is a single-variable knockout. The autopoietic composite (protocell-autopoietic, k_prod=0.03) and the vesicle control (protocell-vesicle-control, k_prod=0.0) share the same seed, the same process wiring, and the same D/k_decay/thr/dt/steps_per_tick; the ONLY difference is whether the internal production loop exists. Measured this build. The closed loop PERSISTS: the enclosed interior area plateaus at ~149 pixels (down from the seed's ~465, throttled by closure -- homeostasis, not runaway growth) and holds there through the ~1800-step run, with persists == 1.0 and collapse_tick == -1.0 (it never breaks within the window) and membrane_mass held above zero. The vesicle control COLLAPSES: with production knocked out, the ring decays and diffuses, its topological closure is lost by step ~96, enclosed_area falls to 0, and persists == 0.0. Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation -- within this authored construction the contrast shows the production loop is what maintains the boundary, not the seed geometry or diffusion alone. What is honestly demonstrated, and what is not. FIRST, the closure criterion is AUTHORED, not emergent: the closure-dependence is hand-coded via a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, so what this study shows is the criterion IMPLEMENTED and its consequences consequence-tested -- not a boundary that made and sustained itself from local physics. Within that authored construction, the production loop is what maintains the boundary, shown two ways -- the k_prod=0 knockout (the single differing variable; it flips persist -> collapse) and the fatal-puncture viability bound (take the closed-loop steady state, zero a wedge of the membrane so nothing is enclosed, and production -- gated on the authored closure test -- cannot rebuild the boundary from nothing; the punctured ring relaxes toward phi=0). That puncture is beer2023's viability bound made concrete -- cross it and the shared state can no longer be maintained. SECOND, the homeostatic plateau is METASTABLE, not eternal, and that is the physically correct signature of finite closure, not a defect. A closed toy system with no external material influx self-maintains over a bounded window (~2000 internal steps, vastly longer than the control's ~96-step collapse) and then eventually runs down; perpetual self-maintenance would require a genuine energy or material influx, and a toy that self-maintained forever from a closed box would be the LESS physical result. THIRD, the reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG: the seed config is accepted but currently inert, so persistence is NOT seed-dependent (unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies), and the multi-seed test's reported range is a degenerate single point (enclosed_area == 149 at every seed 1-5) -- a degenerate single point, not stochastic robustness. FOURTH, this is a minimal self-maintaining boundary, NOT real membrane chemistry or metabolism: what is demonstrated is authored operational closure (the criterion implemented, its knockout and puncture consequences verified), not lipid biophysics, not a metabolic network, not molecular self-production. A mechanistically-local version -- an interior precursor that collapses when the membrane leaks, so closure-dependence follows from local physics rather than a global observer -- is the named next step. Read this side by side with the lumped ODE predecessor, which built the same closure idea as a compiled minimal-cell coupling of metabolism/containment/replication with no boundary-maintenance criterion applied; this study supplies exactly the boundary-maintenance criterion that one named as its own next step, at a very different mechanistic resolution.

Conclusion

Claims

  • The paper's §Self-organized processes autopoiesis criterion is implemented as an AUTHORED operational-closure criterion (production gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, not emergent from local physics) and its consequences are tested on a 2D lattice: a scalar membrane field with production gated on topological closure and self-limited by enclosed area PERSISTS (enclosed_area plateaus ~149 px, persists == 1.0, collapse_tick == -1.0 over 1800 steps), while the single-variable k_prod=0 vesicle control COLLAPSES (enclosed_area -> 0 by step ~96, persists == 0.0). Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation.

  • The consequences of the authored closure criterion are verified two ways -- within this construction persistence tracks the internal production loop: the knockout (the only differing variable) and independently the fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023) -- breaking the boundary so nothing is enclosed shuts off the authored closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild from nothing.

Evidence

  • CLOSED-LOOP-PERSISTS -- persists == 1.0, membrane_mass > 0 over 1800 steps

  • CLOSED-LOOP-ENCLOSES-INTERIOR -- enclosed_area plateaus ~149 px (seed ~465 px), homeostasis

  • CLOSED-LOOP-NEVER-COLLAPSES -- collapse_tick == -1.0 within the window

  • VESICLE-CONTROL-COLLAPSES -- k_prod=0: persists == 0.0 (single-variable knockout)

  • VESICLE-CONTROL-LOSES-ENCLOSURE -- enclosed_area -> 0 by step ~96

  • PUNCTURE-IS-FATAL -- after wedge puncture, enclosed_area stays 0 for 1500 steps, phi -> ~0 (viability bound)

Limitations

  • The closure criterion is AUTHORED, not emergent: closure-dependence is hand-coded via a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, so the knockout and puncture results are consequence-tests of an implemented criterion, not evidence that a boundary made and sustained itself from local physics.

  • The homeostatic plateau is metastable, not eternal -- the physically-correct signature of a closed system with no external material influx (perpetual self-maintenance would need a genuine influx and would be the less physical result), not a defect.

  • The reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG; persistence is deterministic and NOT seed-dependent (the multi-seed range is a degenerate single point, enclosed_area == 149 at every seed), unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies.

  • This is a minimal self-maintaining boundary demonstrating authored operational closure (the criterion implemented, its knockout and puncture consequences verified), NOT real membrane chemistry, a metabolic network, or molecular self-production. Parameters are tuned demonstration values, not fitted to any organism.

Next steps

  • Mechanistically-local closure: replace the global binary_fill_holes observer with local physics in which an interior precursor collapses when the membrane leaks, so closure-dependence follows from the local dynamics rather than an authored global topological test.

  • Graded puncture self-repair: replace the current global-binary closure gate and maintenance-against-decay with a graded response so a partial puncture can be partially repaired -- turning the all-or-nothing viability bound into a recovery gradient.

  • External-influx / open-system autopoiesis: drive the loop with a genuine energy/material influx so self-maintenance is no longer bounded by the closed toy's finite budget, moving from a metastable plateau toward a true non-equilibrium steady state.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • The autopoiesis criterion tested here is the paper's §Self-organized processes one, quoted directly: a membrane alone is insufficient, and the stronger criterion is that the processes inside the boundary collectively maintain the organization that keeps those processes possible. This study realizes that as a production loop GATED on topological closure and self-limited by enclosed area -- the membrane's ability to make boundary material is conditioned on the boundary it maintains. This is operational closure (knockout-provable causation plus a viability bound), not real membrane chemistry, metabolism, or molecular self-production.
  • The closure criterion is AUTHORED (production is gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, not emergent from local physics); within that construction, persistence is a consequence of the internal loop, and this is consequence-tested, not merely assumed. The closed loop (k_prod=0.03) and the vesicle control (k_prod=0.0) differ in exactly one variable -- the production rate constant -- with an identical seed, identical wiring, and identical D/k_decay/thr/dt. The knockout flips persistence into collapse, isolating the loop within the authored construction. A second consequence-test is the fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023): from the closed-loop steady state, zeroing a wedge of the membrane so nothing is enclosed shuts off the authored closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild the boundary from nothing -- it relaxes toward phi=0.
  • The homeostatic plateau is METASTABLE, not perpetual -- and this is the physically-correct signature of finite closure, not a defect to apologize for. A closed toy system with no external material influx self-maintains over a bounded window (~2000 internal steps, versus the control's ~96-step collapse) and eventually runs down (the ring breaks past ~2450-2650 steps even with production on). Both composites pin steps_per_tick=50 and the regression tests cap the closed-loop run at 36 ticks (1800 steps), deliberately inside the plateau window. Perpetual self-maintenance from a closed box would require a genuine energy/material influx and would be the LESS physical result.
  • The reaction-diffusion physics is deterministic: it has NO RNG. The Protocell process accepts a seed config, but it is currently inert (documented in meta_modelers_guide/protocell/autopoiesis.py). So persistence is NOT seed-dependent -- unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies, where seed drives Monte Carlo noise. The multi-seed regression test varies seed 1-5 as the documented interface for a future stochastic variant and as the regression guard that would catch seed silently starting to matter, but today it produces an EXACT invariant: enclosed_area == 149 at every seed. The reported range is therefore a degenerate single point, stated plainly rather than presented as stochastic robustness.
  • The parameters are tuned demonstration values, not fitted. D=0.02 (comfortably inside the mild second-order-diffusion CFL D <= 0.25 -- the CFL guard raises rather than silently admitting an unstable config), k_decay=0.01, k_prod=0.03, thr=0.30 sit in a window that produces a legible homeostatic plateau; they are not fitted to a measured membrane or organism. No literature acceptance band is claimed -- this is a demonstration primitive of the closure criterion, not a model of a specific cell.
  • Persistence is gated on enclosed area > 0 AND membrane mass held above a floor, together, so a degenerate filled-in blob (interior filled solid, area == 0, mass high) or a near-zero-mass ghost ring (area > 0, mass ~0) can never be misread as a persisting vesicle. The enclosed-area metric is topological: a closed ring encloses its center (area > 0), while any gap leaks to the grid border and binary_fill_holes returns the membrane only (area == 0), cleanly separating "there is still a bounded inside" from "the boundary is gone."

Limitations

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • The closure criterion is AUTHORED, not emergent: closure-dependence is hand-coded via a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, so the knockout and puncture results are consequence-tests of an implemented criterion, not evidence that a boundary made and sustained itself from local physics. A mechanistically-local version (an interior precursor that collapses when the membrane leaks) is the named next step.
  • The homeostatic plateau is metastable, not perpetual: a closed toy system with no external material influx self-maintains over a bounded window (~2000 internal steps, versus the control's ~96-step collapse) and eventually runs down. This is the physically-correct signature of finite closure -- perpetual self-maintenance would require a genuine energy/material influx -- not a defect.
  • The reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG. The Protocell seed config is accepted but currently inert, so persistence is deterministic and NOT seed-dependent (unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies). The multi-seed regression's reported range is a degenerate single point (enclosed_area == 149 at every seed 1-5), not stochastic robustness.
  • This is a minimal self-maintaining boundary, NOT real membrane chemistry or metabolism. What is demonstrated is authored operational closure -- the criterion implemented, its consequences verified via the k_prod=0 flip and the fatal puncture -- not lipid biophysics, a metabolic network, or molecular self-production.
  • The parameters (D=0.02, k_decay=0.01, k_prod=0.03, thr=0.30) are tuned demonstration values, not fitted to a measured membrane or organism, and no literature acceptance band is claimed. D=0.02 sits inside the mild second-order-diffusion CFL D <= 0.25; the process raises on an unstable dt rather than silently admitting it.
  • Persistence is a global-binary closure judgement (enclosed area > 0 AND mass held, together): a partial puncture cannot be partially repaired in this build. Graded puncture self-repair is a named next step, not something this study shows.
  • The multi-grain composition idea (the same function realized by conforming handlers at coarse, self-organized, and molecular grains behind one interface) is out of scope; this study addresses containment closure at a single spatial mechanistic grain.
  • The membrane-closure loop is modeled in isolation; coupling it to metabolism and replication through shared component pools (a three-function mutual-closure minimal cell) is a named next step.
