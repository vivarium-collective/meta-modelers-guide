Q The paper's §Self-organized processes section states the autopoiesis criterion as a boundary problem, not a bookkeeping one: "A membrane alone is insufficient: a vesicle may form a boundary without constituting a living system. A stronger criterion is that the processes inside the boundary collectively contribute to maintaining the organization that, in turn, keeps those processes possible." This study puts that criterion on a 2D lattice and asks the discriminating question directly: does a membrane PERSIST because an internal production loop -- gated on the boundary itself staying topologically closed -- replenishes the membrane against decay, and does a mere vesicle with no such loop, from the identical seed, DISSIPATE? The two composites differ in exactly one variable (the production rate constant k_prod), so the contrast between persistence and collapse is the finding. beer2023's viability bounds -- constraints on a shared state that must be maintained for the composition to persist -- are the frame: closure here is that the membrane's own maintenance is conditioned on the boundary it maintains, so break the boundary and the maintenance cannot fire.

Claim

The paper's §Self-organized processes autopoiesis criterion -- a membrane persists because the processes inside the boundary maintain the organization that keeps those processes possible -- is IMPLEMENTED here as an AUTHORED operational-closure criterion and its consequences are tested. The closure-dependence is hand-coded (production is gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer), NOT emergent from local physics; the persistence, knockout, and puncture results all follow from that construction, and are worthwhile because the criterion is implemented and its consequences are verified -- not because closure arose on its own. A scalar membrane field under reaction-diffusion, with production gated on the boundary staying topologically closed and self-limited by enclosed area, PERSISTS: enclosed interior area plateaus at ~149 px (homeostasis, down from the ~465 px seed) with persists == 1.0 and no collapse within a ~1800-step window. The single-variable negative control (k_prod=0, the production loop knocked out, identical seed and wiring) COLLAPSES: closure is lost by step ~96, enclosed_area -> 0, persists == 0.0. Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation, so within this authored construction the production loop is what maintains the boundary -- confirmed independently by a fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023): breaking the boundary so nothing is enclosed shuts off the closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild from nothing. The boundaries of the claim are stated as findings: the homeostatic plateau is metastable, not eternal (the physically-correct signature of a closed system with no external influx, not a defect); the reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG, so persistence is deterministic and NOT seed-dependent (the multi-seed range is a degenerate single point); and this is a minimal self-maintaining boundary, NOT real membrane chemistry or metabolism. The v1 maintenance gate is an authored global topological test, and the mechanistically-local version that removes it is NO LONGER just a named next step -- it is built and measured here as v2 (see below). MECHANISM-LEVEL UPGRADE (v2, ProtocellV2, answering peer-review M4). v2 removes the global observer entirely -- production depends on a LOCAL interior precursor field p, k_prod*phi*p*(1-phi/phi_max), and binary_fill_holes appears ONLY as a passive enclosed_area readout, never in the update (asserted structurally on the update's referenced names). Closure-dependence EMERGES from geometry -- the membrane secretes precursor (s_p*phi) and is a barrier to it (mobility Mp*(1-alpha*phi) drops inside the membrane), so a CLOSED ring confines the precursor it secretes into the enclosed pocket (measured -- interior precursor pools from ~0 to ~72 by step 1000) and sustains itself, while an OPEN ring bleeds it. MEASURED this build -- the v2 closed loop SUSTAINS topological closure ~2451 internal steps (metastable, the same order as v1's ~2450-2650 window, but with no global observer in the loop; enclosed_area declines 465 -> 0 as the membrane thickens inward -- a long metastable transient, not an eternal fixed point), while the PRECURSOR knockout s_p=0 (the new mechanistic single-variable control) collapses at ~step 95, ~26x faster, as does k_prod=0. The PUNCTURE is now a genuine local PREDICTION, not a restatement -- zeroing a 60-degree wedge of the steady ring makes the interior precursor bleed out through the gap and the wedge membrane starves LOCALLY (punctured-wedge mass stays ~2 versus ~79 in the intact ring at the same step) and never self-heals. So EMERGE is upgraded from authored-closure to genuinely-emergent local-mechanism closure. What v2 does NOT do -- reach steady self-maintenance: the plateau is still METASTABLE (a closed budget). The open-system driven version (v2-open, ProtocellV2Open) is BUILT AND MEASURED, and the honest verdict is that a local external drive does NOT sustain closure indefinitely -- it DESTABILISES the ring into faster runaway filling (closure lost ~step 591 vs the undriven ~2451; no local drive in an ~80-point sweep reaches a bounded steady enclosed_area), while the controls confirm the drive does not rescue closure. So EMERGE stays at genuinely-emergent-but-metastable closure, NOT upgraded to steady self-maintenance.

The paper's §Self-organized processes section (its autopoiesis discussion, Fig 9) draws a sharp line a coarser, specification-stage lumped realization could only gesture at: a boundary is not enough. A vesicle forms a boundary and is still not alive; what the criterion asks for is that the processes inside the boundary maintain the very organization that keeps those processes possible. This study realizes that line spatially as a single scalar membrane-density field phi on a 64x64 periodic grid, seeded as a deterministic Gaussian-annulus ring -- a closed membrane -- evolving under a reaction-diffusion update dphi/dt = D*lap(phi) - k_decay*phi + production. The production term is the closure loop made literal: it fires ONLY where the membrane still topologically encloses an interior (binary_fill_holes(phi>thr) & ~(phi>thr)), is deposited onto existing membrane, and is throttled by the enclosed area relative to the seed's own enclosed area, so the loop self-limits into a homeostatic plateau rather than growing without bound. The membrane's ability to make boundary material is conditioned on the boundary it maintains -- exactly beer2023's viability-bound reading of closure, where a shared state must be held for the composition to persist. The discriminator is a single-variable knockout. The autopoietic composite (protocell-autopoietic, k_prod=0.03) and the vesicle control (protocell-vesicle-control, k_prod=0.0) share the same seed, the same process wiring, and the same D/k_decay/thr/dt/steps_per_tick; the ONLY difference is whether the internal production loop exists. Measured this build. The closed loop PERSISTS: the enclosed interior area plateaus at ~149 pixels (down from the seed's ~465, throttled by closure -- homeostasis, not runaway growth) and holds there through the ~1800-step run, with persists == 1.0 and collapse_tick == -1.0 (it never breaks within the window) and membrane_mass held above zero. The vesicle control COLLAPSES: with production knocked out, the ring decays and diffuses, its topological closure is lost by step ~96, enclosed_area falls to 0, and persists == 0.0. Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation -- within this authored construction the contrast shows the production loop is what maintains the boundary, not the seed geometry or diffusion alone. What is honestly demonstrated, and what is not. FIRST, the closure criterion is AUTHORED, not emergent: the closure-dependence is hand-coded via a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, so what this study shows is the criterion IMPLEMENTED and its consequences consequence-tested -- not a boundary that made and sustained itself from local physics. Within that authored construction, the production loop is what maintains the boundary, shown two ways -- the k_prod=0 knockout (the single differing variable; it flips persist -> collapse) and the fatal-puncture viability bound (take the closed-loop steady state, zero a wedge of the membrane so nothing is enclosed, and production -- gated on the authored closure test -- cannot rebuild the boundary from nothing; the punctured ring relaxes toward phi=0). That puncture is beer2023's viability bound made concrete -- cross it and the shared state can no longer be maintained. SECOND, the homeostatic plateau is METASTABLE, not eternal, and that is the physically correct signature of finite closure, not a defect. A closed toy system with no external material influx self-maintains over a bounded window (~2000 internal steps, vastly longer than the control's ~96-step collapse) and then eventually runs down; perpetual self-maintenance would require a genuine energy or material influx, and a toy that self-maintained forever from a closed box would be the LESS physical result. THIRD, the reaction-diffusion physics has no RNG: the seed config is accepted but currently inert, so persistence is NOT seed-dependent (unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies), and the multi-seed test's reported range is a degenerate single point (enclosed_area == 149 at every seed 1-5) -- a degenerate single point, not stochastic robustness. FOURTH, this is a minimal self-maintaining boundary, NOT real membrane chemistry or metabolism: what is demonstrated is operational closure (the criterion implemented, its knockout and puncture consequences verified), not lipid biophysics, not a metabolic network, not molecular self-production. These four boundaries describe the v1 SPECIFICATION-level realization. The mechanistically-local version they named -- an interior precursor confined by a closed membrane that bleeds out when the membrane leaks, so closure-dependence follows from LOCAL physics rather than a global observer -- is NO longer a next step: it is built and measured here as v2 (protocell-autopoietic-v2, ProtocellV2), answering peer-review M4. v2 removes the binary_fill_holes observer from the update entirely (production depends on the local precursor field p; the observer survives only as a passive enclosed_area readout, asserted structurally absent from the update). MEASURED -- the v2 closed loop sustains topological closure ~2451 internal steps (persists == 1.0, enclosed_area ~293 px at 1000 steps, interior precursor pooled ~72) with no global observer in the loop, while the mechanistic precursor knockout s_p=0 collapses at ~step 95 (~26x faster) and a 60-degree wedge puncture makes the interior precursor bleed out through the gap so the wedge membrane starves LOCALLY (~2 vs ~79 intact) and never self-heals. So EMERGE is upgraded from authored-closure (v1) to genuinely-emergent local-mechanism closure (v2); v2's plateau is still metastable on a finite closed budget. The open-system driven version (v2-open, ProtocellV2Open, finding F-04) is now BUILT AND MEASURED, and the honest verdict is that a local external drive does NOT make emergent closure sustain indefinitely -- it DESTABILISES the metastable ring into faster runaway filling (closure lost ~step 591 at the canonical drive vs ~2451 undriven; an ~80-point drive sweep found no local drive reaching a bounded steady enclosed_area), while the controls confirm the drive does not rescue closure (s_p=0 still collapses ~step 188, a puncture is not healed). EMERGE therefore stays at genuinely-emergent-but-metastable closure, NOT upgraded to steady self-maintenance -- an honest measure of how hard steady emergent closure is. Read this side by side with the lumped ODE predecessor, which built the same closure idea as a compiled minimal-cell coupling of metabolism/containment/replication with no boundary-maintenance criterion applied; this study supplies exactly the boundary-maintenance criterion that one named as its own next step, at a very different mechanistic resolution.

Result

The autopoietic closed loop persists -- enclosed interior area plateaus at ~149 px (homeostasis, down from the ~465 px seed) with persists == 1.0 and collapse_tick == -1.0 over 1800 steps -- while the single-variable k_prod=0 vesicle control collapses (enclosed_area -> 0 by step ~96, persists == 0.0), implementing the paper's §Self-organized processes autopoiesis criterion as an AUTHORED operational-closure criterion (gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, not emergent from local physics) and testing its consequences. Within this authored construction persistence tracks the internal loop, verified by the knockout (the only differing variable) and independently by a fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023) -- breaking the boundary shuts off the authored closure-gated production and the ring cannot rebuild from nothing. The MECHANISM-level v2 (protocell-autopoietic-v2, ProtocellV2) then removes the global observer entirely and reproduces closure from LOCAL physics -- production depends on a local interior precursor confined by the closed membrane -- so closure-dependence EMERGES -- the v2 closed loop sustains closure ~2451 internal steps (persists == 1.0, enclosed_area ~293 px, precursor pooled ~72) with no binary_fill_holes in the update (asserted structurally), the precursor knockout s_p=0 collapses at ~step 95, and a wedge puncture makes the interior precursor bleed out so the wedge membrane starves locally (~2 vs ~79 intact), never self-healing. EMERGE is upgraded from authored-closure (v1) to genuinely-emergent local-mechanism closure (v2). The open-system driven version (v2-open, ProtocellV2Open, F-04) is then BUILT AND MEASURED, and the honest verdict is that a local external drive does NOT convert the metastable ring into steady self-maintenance -- it DESTABILISES it into faster runaway filling (closure lost ~step 591 vs undriven ~2451; no local drive in an ~80-point sweep reaches a bounded steady enclosed_area), with the controls confirming the drive does not rescue closure (s_p=0 collapses ~step 188, a puncture is not healed). So EMERGE stays at genuinely-emergent-but-metastable closure. Remaining boundaries are reported as limitations -- v2''s plateau is still metastable on a finite closed budget and external drive does not fix it (the window also depends on seed geometry), the physics is deterministic so persistence is not seed-dependent (the multi-seed range is a degenerate single point), and this is a minimal self-maintaining boundary, not real membrane chemistry or metabolism.

Conclusion

Claims

  • The paper's §Self-organized processes autopoiesis criterion is implemented as an AUTHORED operational-closure criterion (production gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, not emergent from local physics) and its consequences are tested on a 2D lattice: a scalar membrane field with production gated on topological closure and self-limited by enclosed area PERSISTS (enclosed_area plateaus ~149 px, persists == 1.0, collapse_tick == -1.0 over 1800 steps), while the single-variable k_prod=0 vesicle control COLLAPSES (enclosed_area -> 0 by step ~96, persists == 0.0). Flipping one rate constant flips persistence into dissipation.

  • The consequences of the authored closure criterion are verified two ways -- within this construction persistence tracks the internal production loop: the knockout (the only differing variable) and independently the fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023) -- breaking the boundary so nothing is enclosed shuts off the authored closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild from nothing.

  • MECHANISM-LEVEL EMERGE UPGRADE (v2, ProtocellV2, answering peer-review M4): removing the global binary_fill_holes observer from the update and making production depend on a LOCAL interior precursor yields GENUINELY EMERGENT closure. EMERGE is upgraded from authored-closure to genuinely-emergent local-mechanism closure.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • The autopoiesis criterion tested here is the paper's §Self-organized processes one, quoted directly: a membrane alone is insufficient, and the stronger criterion is that the processes inside the boundary collectively maintain the organization that keeps those processes possible. This study realizes that as a production loop GATED on topological closure and self-limited by enclosed area -- the membrane's ability to make boundary material is conditioned on the boundary it maintains. This is operational closure (knockout-provable causation plus a viability bound), not real membrane chemistry, metabolism, or molecular self-production.
  • The closure criterion is AUTHORED (production is gated by a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, not emergent from local physics); within that construction, persistence is a consequence of the internal loop, and this is consequence-tested, not merely assumed. The closed loop (k_prod=0.03) and the vesicle control (k_prod=0.0) differ in exactly one variable -- the production rate constant -- with an identical seed, identical wiring, and identical D/k_decay/thr/dt. The knockout flips persistence into collapse, isolating the loop within the authored construction. A second consequence-test is the fatal-puncture viability bound (beer2023): from the closed-loop steady state, zeroing a wedge of the membrane so nothing is enclosed shuts off the authored closure-gated production, and the ring cannot rebuild the boundary from nothing -- it relaxes toward phi=0.
  • The homeostatic plateau is METASTABLE, not perpetual -- and this is the physically-correct signature of finite closure, not a defect to apologize for. A closed toy system with no external material influx self-maintains over a bounded window (~2000 internal steps, versus the control's ~96-step collapse) and eventually runs down (the ring breaks past ~2450-2650 steps even with production on). Both composites pin steps_per_tick=50 and the regression tests cap the closed-loop run at 36 ticks (1800 steps), deliberately inside the plateau window. Perpetual self-maintenance from a closed box would require a genuine energy/material influx and would be the LESS physical result.
  • The reaction-diffusion physics is deterministic: it has NO RNG. The Protocell process accepts a seed config, but it is currently inert (documented in meta_modelers_guide/protocell/autopoiesis.py). So persistence is NOT seed-dependent -- unlike this investigation's CPM/Metropolis studies, where seed drives Monte Carlo noise. The multi-seed regression test varies seed 1-5 as the documented interface for a future stochastic variant and as the regression guard that would catch seed silently starting to matter, but today it produces an EXACT invariant: enclosed_area == 149 at every seed. The reported range is therefore a degenerate single point, stated plainly rather than presented as stochastic robustness.
  • The parameters are tuned demonstration values, not fitted. D=0.02 (comfortably inside the mild second-order-diffusion CFL D <= 0.25 -- the CFL guard raises rather than silently admitting an unstable config), k_decay=0.01, k_prod=0.03, thr=0.30 sit in a window that produces a legible homeostatic plateau; they are not fitted to a measured membrane or organism. No literature acceptance band is claimed -- this is a demonstration primitive of the closure criterion, not a model of a specific cell.
  • Persistence is gated on enclosed area > 0 AND membrane mass held above a floor, together, so a degenerate filled-in blob (interior filled solid, area == 0, mass high) or a near-zero-mass ghost ring (area > 0, mass ~0) can never be misread as a persisting vesicle. The enclosed-area metric is topological: a closed ring encloses its center (area > 0), while any gap leaks to the grid border and binary_fill_holes returns the membrane only (area == 0), cleanly separating "there is still a bounded inside" from "the boundary is gone."

What this study explicitly does NOT validate (prevents overclaim).

  • The v1 closure criterion (protocell-autopoietic) is AUTHORED, not emergent: closure-dependence is hand-coded via a global binary_fill_holes topological observer, so its knockout and puncture results are consequence-tests of an implemented criterion.
