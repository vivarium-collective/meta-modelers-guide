Q The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 6) frames a molecule not as a lump of state but as a typed interface exposing several physical channels -- chemical, electrostatic, mechanical, thermal -- through which molecules bind, react, and transmit force and heat. It even names the public databases (PDB, Reactome, ChEBI, GO) as "partial specifications of molecular interfaces and their types". This study asks the discriminating question one channel down: does that molecular interface hold SPATIALLY? Concretely -- do local molecular reactions plus differential diffusion (the CHEMICAL channel spatialized as finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion patterning -- not linear-instability Turing sensu stricto) turn a near-uniform seed into emergent spatial structure, and does a static uniform temperature field acting as an Arrhenius rate multiplier (no heat transport, no reaction->heat feedback) GRADE that structure -- so that a temperature PARAMETER demonstrably modulates the spatialized chemical channel over one shared spatial field? The causal control -- equal diffusion (Du = Dv), chemistry untouched -- differs in exactly one variable and isolates the diffusion asymmetry as the cause of structure (a regime-specific conclusion for this Gray-Scott system), so the contrast between pattern and uniformity is the finding.

Claim

The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 6) holds spatially for its CHEMICAL channel, and a temperature parameter modulates it. The CHEMICAL channel, realized as a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion system (local autocatalysis U + 2V -> 3V plus differential diffusion Dv < Du), turns a near-uniform seed into an emergent finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven Gray-Scott spot pattern (not linear-instability Turing sensu stricto): v_var rises from ~0.0011 to ~0.0119 with ~12 connected domains (patterned == 1.0). The equal-diffusion causal control (Du = Dv = 0.12, chemistry unchanged -- the single differing variable) stays UNIFORM (v_var ~= 0, patterned == 0.0), so the pattern is CAUSED by the diffusion asymmetry, not the chemistry alone -- a regime-specific conclusion for this Gray-Scott system. A static uniform temperature field acting as an Arrhenius rate multiplier exp(-Ea*(1/T - 1/Tref)) (no heat transport, no reaction->heat feedback) GRADES the pattern (Ea=0.15, T=1.2: ~12 domains -> ~2, v_var ~0.0119 -> ~0.0096) while staying patterned: a temperature parameter modulating the chemical channel, not a second physically-transported channel and not a collapse. Multi-seed (seeds 1-5, physics is deterministic so the seed is re-drawn into the initial field): v_var in [0.01157, 0.01193], n_domains in [8, 12] -- reported as a range. The boundaries are findings, not caveats: the ELECTROSTATIC and MECHANICAL channels are the named four-modality gap; the databases claim (PDB/Reactome/ChEBI/GO as partial specifications of molecular-interface types) is realized as an ILLUSTRATIVE typed-port annotation over the Gray-Scott species, not a resolved mapping; and this is a toy reaction-diffusion demonstration, not real molecular structure or conformation.

The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 6) makes a molecule a typed interface: it exposes physical channels -- chemical fluxes, electrostatic fields, mechanical forces, thermal transfer -- and its identity is the shape of those channels, not a scalar concentration. A coarser, specification-stage, point-scale lumped realization made ONE molecule's four channels executable at a POINT (an F1Fo ATP synthase whose chemical/electrostatic/mechanical/thermal ports were wired as a typed draft and compiled). This study takes the CHEMICAL channel off the point and onto a 2D lattice, and adds a static uniform temperature field on top of it, to ask whether a temperature PARAMETER can modulate the spatialized chemical channel over one shared field. The chemical channel is realized as a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion system on a 128x128 periodic grid: a local autocatalytic chemistry (U + 2V -> 3V, V -> P) coupled to DIFFERENTIAL diffusion (Dv < Du), the route by which a near-uniform Gray-Scott state, seeded with finite-amplitude nucleation patches, breaks into spots -- finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven patterning, NOT the linear-instability Turing mechanism sensu stricto (cf. turing1952). The update is explicit-Euler du/dt = Du*lap(u) - U*V^2 + F*(1-U), dv/dt = Dv*lap(v) + U*V^2 - (F+k)*V on the periodic 5-point Laplacian, canonical spot regime Du=0.16, Dv=0.08, F=0.037, k=0.06, dt=1.0 (CFL max(Du,Dv)*dt*4 = 0.64 < 1, guarded at construction). The thermal channel is a second, orthogonal port: when a static uniform temperature field is present the reaction term is scaled elementwise by the Arrhenius factor rate = exp(-Ea*(1/T - 1/Tref)) -- T above Tref speeds the chemistry, below slows it, and rate == 1 with no field. There is no heat transport and no reaction->heat feedback: the temperature field is a static rate MULTIPLIER, so the temperature parameter modulates the chemical channel additively rather than being a second physically-transported channel. Realizing the paper's databases claim, honestly. The paper calls PDB/Reactome/ChEBI/GO "partial specifications of molecular interfaces and their types"; this study makes that concrete not as a resolved database lookup but as an ILLUSTRATIVE typed-port annotation over the Gray-Scott species and reactions. Read U as a ChEBI-style substrate type, V as an activator/inhibitor species, P as the product sink, and U + 2V -> 3V as a Reactome-style autocatalytic reaction record (placeholder identifiers, e.g. CHEBI:<U>/CHEBI:<V>/CHEBI:<P> and REACT:<autocatalysis>) -- a concrete gesture at what it would mean for a database entry to TYPE a molecular interface, not a claim that these toy species resolve to real PDB structures or Reactome pathway fragments. A genuine Reactome-fragment mapping, or a live database fetch that types the ports against real entries, is a named deferral, not a result here. Measured this build. The CHEMICAL channel patterns: from the near-uniform seed the inhibitor field's spatial variance v_var rises from ~0.0011 toward ~0.0119 by 8000 internal steps and the field resolves into ~12 connected v-domains (patterned == 1.0). The equal-diffusion causal control (Du = Dv = 0.12, chemistry identical) stays UNIFORM -- v_var ~= 0 (patterned == 0.0) -- so the structure is caused by the diffusion asymmetry (a finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven instability, not linear-Turing sensu stricto), not by the chemistry alone: removing only the differential diffusion removes the pattern (a regime-specific conclusion for this Gray-Scott system). The THERMAL channel GRADES that pattern rather than destroying it: a raised uniform temperature field (Ea=0.15, Tref=1.0, T=1.2) coarsens the flagship from ~12 domains to ~2 and lowers v_var from ~0.0119 to ~0.0096 while staying comfortably patterned -- a temperature parameter modulating the chemical channel, not a chemistry collapse. (An earlier, hotter setting Ea=1.0/T=1.5 was found to OVERSHOOT -- the reaction runs away and collapses the pattern to near-uniform -- and was retuned to the milder graded setting; the overshoot is pinned as a direct-physics assertion so "graded, not collapsed" is tested against its own failure mode.) Because the physics has no RNG, the multi-seed check re-draws the near-uniform seed for seeds 1-5 and reports a RANGE: v_var in [0.01157, 0.01193], n_domains in [8, 12] -- the pattern forms for every seed, stated as a band rather than a single golden value. Scope -- the four-modality finding. This study spatializes ONE of the molecular interface's four physical channels -- the CHEMICAL channel (finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion patterning) -- and modulates it with a temperature PARAMETER (a static uniform temperature field acting as an Arrhenius rate multiplier; no heat transport, no reaction->heat feedback). The ELECTROSTATIC and MECHANICAL channels are the named gap -- no charge field, no force or tension, is exposed or measured here (owned in investigation.yaml's what_this_does_not_demonstrate, as a named deferral). And this is a TOY reaction-diffusion demonstration: emergent spatial structure from a minimal autocatalytic chemistry with tuned, uncalibrated constants, NOT real molecular structure or conformation (nothing at PDB resolution), NOT a fitted reaction network, NOT a resolved database mapping. What is demonstrated is that a temperature parameter modulates the spatialized chemical channel over one shared field (one physical channel modulated by a temperature parameter, not two physically-transported channels composing); what is not is the other channels, real molecular structure, and a live database type system.

Conclusion

Claims

  • The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 6) chemical channel holds as a 2D spatial mechanism: a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion system (autocatalysis U + 2V -> 3V plus differential diffusion Dv < Du) turns a near-uniform seed into an emergent finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven Gray-Scott spot pattern (not linear-instability Turing sensu stricto; v_var ~0.0011 -> ~0.0119, ~12 domains, patterned == 1.0), while the single-variable equal-diffusion causal control (Du = Dv = 0.12) stays UNIFORM (v_var ~= 0, patterned == 0.0). Removing only the diffusion asymmetry removes the pattern (a regime-specific conclusion for this Gray-Scott system).

  • A temperature parameter modulates the chemical channel: a static uniform temperature field acting as an Arrhenius rate multiplier (Ea=0.15, T=1.2; no heat transport, no reaction->heat feedback) GRADES the pattern (~12 -> ~2 domains, v_var ~0.0119 -> ~0.0096) while staying patterned -- a temperature parameter modulating one physical channel, not a second physically-transported channel and not a collapse.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • The interface tested here is the paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 6): a molecule as a typed interface exposing physical channels (chemical, electrostatic, mechanical, thermal). This study realizes the CHEMICAL channel spatially as Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion (local autocatalysis U + 2V -> 3V, V -> P plus differential diffusion Dv < Du -- finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven patterning, not linear-instability Turing sensu stricto; cf. turing1952) and adds a temperature PARAMETER as a static uniform temperature field acting as an Arrhenius elementwise rate multiplier exp(-Ea*(1/T - 1/Tref)) on the reaction term (no heat transport, no reaction->heat feedback). The ELECTROSTATIC and MECHANICAL channels are NOT realized -- they are the named four-modality gap, not demonstrated here.
  • The databases hook is ILLUSTRATIVE typed-port annotation, not a resolved mapping. The paper names PDB/Reactome/ChEBI/GO as "partial specifications of molecular interfaces and their types"; here that is gestured at by TYPING the Gray-Scott species and reaction with placeholder database-style identifiers (U/V/P as ChEBI-style substrate/activator/product types, U + 2V -> 3V as a Reactome-style autocatalytic reaction record). This is a concrete gesture at what typing a molecular interface against a database would mean -- NOT a claim that these toy species resolve to real PDB structures, Reactome pathway fragments, or ChEBI/GO entries. A real Reactome-fragment mapping / live database fetch is a deferral.
  • The pattern is CAUSED by the differential-diffusion instability (finite-amplitude, nucleation-driven, not linear-Turing sensu stricto), and this is shown by a single-variable causal control, not assumed. The flagship (Du=0.16, Dv=0.08) and the equal-diffusion control (Du=Dv=0.12) share identical chemistry (F, k), identical seed, identical wiring and step count; the ONLY difference is whether diffusion is differential. Removing the asymmetry removes the pattern (v_var ~= 0, exactly 0.0 in the control), isolating differential diffusion -- not the reaction alone -- as the cause of spatial structure.
  • The temperature parameter modulates the chemical channel additively, not as a re-parameterization of it. The reaction term is scaled by rate = exp(-Ea*(1/T - 1/Tref)); with NO temperature field present rate == 1 everywhere and the process is pure chemistry, byte-identical to the chemical-only flagship. The thermal composite differs from the flagship in exactly the Arrhenius parameters and the presence of a raised static uniform temperature field -- Du/Dv/F/k/dt/steps_per_tick are held equal (asserted in tests/test_molecular_regime.py), so the coarsening is attributable to the temperature parameter, not to a changed chemistry or diffusion regime. The temperature field is a static rate multiplier with no heat transport and no reaction->heat feedback -- this is one physical channel modulated by a temperature parameter, not two physically-transported channels composing.
  • The parameters are tuned demonstration values, not fitted. The canonical spot regime (Du=0.16, Dv=0.08, F=0.037, k=0.06, dt=1.0) sits in the classic Gray-Scott spot window and comfortably inside the explicit reaction-diffusion CFL limit (max(Du,Dv)*dt*4 = 0.64 < 1; the process raises on an unstable config rather than drifting silently to NaN). The thermal setting (Ea=0.15, T=1.2) was chosen to GRADE rather than collapse: a hotter Ea=1.0/T=1.5 was found to overshoot into near-uniform collapse and was retuned. No literature acceptance band is claimed -- this is a demonstration primitive of the molecular interface's chemical and thermal channels made spatial, not a model of a specific molecule or medium.
  • The physics is DETERMINISTIC -- it has no RNG. The GrayScott process accepts a seed config key but it is currently inert (documented in meta_modelers_guide/molecular/gray_scott.py), so the composites bake a static seed_uv(seed=1) draw into fields.u/fields.v at build time. The multi-seed test therefore re-draws the INITIAL near-uniform field with seed_uv(seed=s) for s in 1-5 (everything else held fixed) and reports the resulting band -- v_var in [0.01157, 0.01193], n_domains in [8, 12] -- as genuine sensitivity of the emergent pattern to the nucleation-patch placement, not as a per-tick stochastic process.
