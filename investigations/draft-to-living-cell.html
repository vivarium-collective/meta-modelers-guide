Introduction

This investigation turns A meta-modeler's guide to the cellular interface into an executable test of compositional modeling. Each figure of the paper is written twice. First as a draft: a typed, unit-bearing biological interface — a STRUCTURAL contract of port names, types, and wiring, with no committed mechanism. It says what a process couples to, not how. Then it is compiled, which installs one or more conforming mechanisms — mechanistic, rule-based, data-driven, or otherwise — behind the identical ports, so the running simulation preserves the declared interface exactly. The compiler's conformance check is STRUCTURAL: it verifies that a handler supplies every port with a compatible type and wiring, and refuses one that does not. It does not check units, dimensions, invariants, or runtime behavior.

The point is not to assemble a single monolithic cell model. It is to test whether a biological model can remain open-ended as its mechanisms change, its assumptions fail, and its level of description shifts. Interfaces provide the places where models can be connected, isolated, replaced, or expanded; graph rewrites allow the composition itself to change through events such as growth, division, and disintegration.

The investigation therefore follows the paper's central claim: composition is not the final architecture of a model, but an ongoing way of building one. Across the studies below, we ask whether the same biological specification can support alternative mechanisms, whether those mechanisms can be composed without losing their interface semantics, and whether the resulting system can reorganize as the biology demands. The endpoint is a composed whole cell that grows, divides, loses viability, and changes its own level of description — demonstrating the full arc from interface specification to living, changing simulation.

One interface, three mechanisms

One metabolism interface — nutrients ⇒ biomass — realized three ways: a first-order lumped yield, saturating Michaelis–Menten kinetics, and a real COBRApy flux-balance LP. The compiler installs each behind the identical ports, and the three produce three genuinely different trajectories — biomass 4.0 (linear), 2.67 (saturating), and 6.29 (with an acetate overflow of 30.4 on the secretions port the FBA solver discovers on its own). Same interface, interchangeable mechanism, distinct dynamics — handler independence (law 4) made runnable.

INTERFACE PRESERVED THE THESIS — one nutrients⇒biomass interface, three conforming handlers → coarse 4.0 / kinetic 2.67 / real-FBA 6.29 (+acetate overflow 30.4); a non-conforming impostor is rejected at compile time. Same ports, distinct dynamics (law 4)

Evidence for

  • Fig 4 — the cellular interface is a set of typed, unit-bearing ports (chemical mol·s⁻¹, mechanical N, electrical A, thermal W, growth hr⁻¹, viability); a draft declares them, a handler makes a bounded goal-directed cell out of them.
  • Fig 5 — cell–environment coupling closes as a sense/act loop over a shared field store; the executable diffuses a real spatial (map[float]) chemical field.
  • Fig 6 (THE THESIS) — one metabolism interface (nutrients ⇒ biomass, energy, entropy, secretions) is realized by a coarse process, a saturating-kinetic process, OR real COBRApy FBA — three mechanisms, one interface (handler independence, law 4). The headline control is a non-conforming impostor handler, REJECTED at compile time with a CompileError naming the missing ports (structural interface preservation, law 2).
  • Fig 8 — molecular composition is a six-level-deep nested place graph (ECM → membrane → cytoplasm → nucleus → chromosome …) with a coupled gene-expression cascade wired to its leaves; the interface survives the deepest nesting (law 2).
  • Fig 9 — a toy autocatalytic loop of metabolism, containment, and replication compiles and runs at coarse, self-organized, and molecular grains; it illustrates the closure PATTERN but is a stand-in, not a demonstration of autopoiesis (folded into the gallery, deflated).
  • Fig 10 — division is a FIRST-CLASS structural rewrite. The cell's own replicated DNA crosses a threshold (dna 1 → 3.06, threshold 2.0) and one cell node genuinely becomes two, conserving mass (the parent is partitioned to daughters, not duplicated). Fig 10-2 development and Fig 10-3 evolution also compile and run, but are deflated to the gallery — their selection is an ODE and their new port a config flag flip.
  • The whole cell (mechanism-swap at scale) — an assembled whole cell (built by hand in the figures' style, NOT compiled from the drafts) runs the Fig 6 mechanism-swap. Coarse, kinetic, and real-FBA metabolism behind one interface give three distinct life histories (peak biomass 5.78 / 6.43 / 6.05). Each grows, divides once (mass-conserved), and dies on a scripted thermal shock (37 → 50 °C at t=12) that collapses viability to 0.018 — law 4 carried from a single figure up to a composed cell.

Evidence against

  • The figures are toy-real, not calibrated — handlers use plausible constants, not fitted ones, so the dynamics demonstrate the pattern (ultrasensitivity, closure, division, disintegration) rather than a quantitatively validated cell.
  • Fig 4a (interaction modalities) is illustrative — a vocabulary of ports, not a mechanism — and remains draft-only by design.

Method & shared caveats

Every figure begins as an inert draft — typed, unit-bearing ports and a behavior contract, no dynamics — and is compiled by installing one conforming mechanism, which preserves the interface exactly (compiler law 2) while giving the figure real dynamics. Readouts below are measured, not asserted: every executable and the whole cell were run and their observables recorded.

Limitations (shared across every study)

  • Two layers, read deliberately: the DRAFT composites are inert (ports + contract, no dynamics) and are rendered in loom; the EXECUTABLE composites run and are shown as dynamics figures. A study's draft render and its executable dynamics are the same interface at two stages, not two different models.
  • The compiler is the binding stage only — it installs a conforming mechanism per draft; it does not fit parameters or invent mechanisms. Conformance is STRUCTURAL (port names, types, wiring), not behavioral — it does not check units, dimensions, invariants, or runtime behavior.
  • The capstone whole cell (the-living-atlas) is ASSEMBLED BY HAND in the figures' style, NOT emitted by the compiler from the figure drafts. Its death is a SCRIPTED thermal shock (hard-coded t=12), not an emergent failure; its division conserves mass (the mother is halved, not doubled).
