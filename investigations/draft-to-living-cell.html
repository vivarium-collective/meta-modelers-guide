Executable atlas · A meta-modeler's guide to the cellular interface

From Draft to Living Cell

This investigation turns the cellular-interface paper's nine composition patterns into executable, behavior-gated interface tests. Each pattern is a hypothesis about which interactions matter, expressed as one typed coupling process; running the composite either reproduces the pattern's characteristic behavior or it does not. Read in order, the nine studies trace an arc — interface, individuality, viability, self-maintenance, agency, adaptation — from the bare interface contract to a growing, competing, self-maintaining, evolving agent. These are demonstrations, not fitted models: every constant is toy-real, chosen for legible dynamics over a short run, and every study states its honest gap. Process bigraphs and Vivarium are the example substrate, not the thesis — the thesis is that interfaces are a natural level at which heterogeneous mechanisms compose and can be compared. The flagship, cell-environment-coupling, makes the outward sense/act loop concrete: one coupling process reads a spatio-flux nutrient grid at a real CPM cell's own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step there (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows the cell's CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes the uptake and secretion back into the shared field as a spatial delta that spatio-flux's DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks the cell grows roughly 3.4x (32 to 110 CPM lattice pixels on a 60x60 field), depletes the glucose at its own footprint by up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and leaves a spreading acetate plume — niche construction made spatial and metabolic, not scripted. All nine patterns now have a study: eight realized as real 2D spatial mechanisms — most as compositions of two independently-built simulators, a few as a single spatial reaction-diffusion or phase-field mechanism — plus the foundational cellular-interface contract itself. Learning — a cell whose history of interaction changes its future regulation to keep itself viable — is the arc's open next rung, named but not yet built.

Introduction

"From Draft to Living Cell" is the executable companion to the meta-modeler's guide: the paper's nine cellular composition patterns, each realized as a runnable model behind one typed interface. The organizing idea is the paper's own — an interface is a modeling choice and a testable biological hypothesis at once, naming which interactions across a cell's boundary matter and which can be abstracted away. Every study makes one such interface explicit and puts it behind a behavior gate. The interface is the load-bearing artifact: it, not either framework alone, is what lets two simulators that were never designed with each other in mind compose. The flagship's coupling process, CpmCellField, is exactly this — the point where a CPM cell's footprint (a set of lattice pixels) becomes a spatio-flux field read, and a dynamic-FBA solution (mmol/gDW/hr fluxes) becomes a per-pixel field delta and a CPM target-volume update. Composition runs in two directions: outward, where a cell couples to its environment and to other cells; and inward, where internal processes compose to produce the interface and the viability constraints that hold it together. Process bigraphs and Vivarium are the example substrate that makes all of this executable — they are how the patterns are expressed, not the claim being tested.

The paper's substitutability claim — that different mechanisms may realize the same externally observable relation, so interfaces provide a natural level at which heterogeneous mechanisms can be compared — is shown concretely rather than asserted: the flagship swaps its internal metabolism from dynamic-FBA to a lumped Michaelis-Menten kinetic behind the byte-identical CpmCellField ports, and the interface-level observables agree within ~10%. Same interface, different mechanism, preserved behavior.

Evidence for

  • cell-environment-coupling (the outward sense/act flagship) — a real CPM cell reads a spatio-flux glucose field at its own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows its CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes uptake/secretion back into the field as a spatial delta that DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks: CPM volume grows ~3.4x (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of the 60x60 lattice — the cell does not fill it), local footprint glucose depletes up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and the field-wide acetate total rises from 0 to ~47 field units as the secreted plume diffuses outward. Net field-wide glucose falls (~32 of 5940 total field mass consumed) even though the depletion signal is much sharper locally at the cell's own footprint than in the field-wide total — the direct niche-construction signature.
  • Substitutability, run concretely — the flagship's internal metabolism is swapped from dynamic-FBA to a lumped Michaelis-Menten kinetic behind the byte-identical CpmCellField ports, and the interface-level observables (growth, local depletion, secretion) agree within ~10%. Same interface, different mechanism, preserved behavior: the paper's equivalence-class claim made executable.

Evidence against

  • Each study realizes only part of its pattern's full claim: two of the four physical modalities (mechanical, electrical) are unrealized investigation-wide; the composition is framework-level, not per-cell; and learning, the arc's next rung, is not yet built.
  • Chemotaxis (directed up-gradient motion) is deferred; the flagship agent senses and acts on the field locally but does not move toward higher concentration.
  • The e_coli_core constants (glucose_km, glucose_vmax, oxygen_vmax, box_volume_L, grow_per_biomass) are toy-real — plausible, hand-tuned magnitudes chosen to produce gradual, spatially-legible dynamics over a short run, not fitted to any organism or medium.
  • The CPM lattice itself (the cell's shape, occupied pixels, footprint) lives inside viva-cpm's Rust engine as a private, write-protected world object, not a process-bigraph store — only the coupling process reads and writes it. The environment the interface exposes to the rest of the composite is exclusively the spatio-flux fields store (glucose, acetate).

The Cellular Interface Accepted

The cell's interface is authored as an inert typed contract and compiled to a running bounded cell; a non-conforming handler that breaks the port contract is rejected at compile time with a CompileError naming the missing ports (Law 1 conformance).

Question & findings

Question

Can the cellular boundary be specified as nothing but typed, unit-bearing exchange ports (chemical mol·s⁻¹, mechanical N, electrical C·s⁻¹, thermal J·s⁻¹) plus higher-level variables (growth, shape, objective, viability) — with no committed mechanism — and compiled, by installing one conforming handler, into a bounded, goal-directed cell whose interface is exactly the one declared?

Findings

  • mechanism Installing one conforming handler grows the cell through its interface. Confirmed: shape 1.0 → 4.2.
  • observation The cell takes up chemical across its interface (net negative flux). Confirmed: chemical flux 0.0 → -0.8.
  • mechanism The cell makes progress toward its declared objective as growth proceeds. Confirmed: objective 0.0 → 1.6.
  • mechanism Law 1 (conformance) is machine-checked at compile time: a handler whose declared ports do not match the draft signature is rejected with a CompileError naming the missing ports. The one concrete impostor in the codebase, NonConformingMetabolism, breaks the metabolism interface used by the disintegration/Fig 6 study (not a cellular-interface-specific contract), but it is the mechanical proof that the compiler enforces the same Law-1 structural check for any declared interface, including this one — no cellular-interface-specific impostor exists, and none is claimed here.
02

Cell–Environment Coupling (flagship) Accepted

Composing a real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField) reproduces the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as genuine spatial metabolism, not a scripted stand-in for it (the loop is closed; directed control/chemotaxis is deferred): over 20 ticks the cell runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint (e_coli_core, O2-capped to force acetate overflow), grows ~3.4x in CPM volume (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of a 60x60 lattice it does not come close to filling), depletes local glucose by up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it (niche construction), and secretes a diffusing acetate plume — two independently-built simulators talking through one typed port.

Question & findings

Question

When a single cell metabolizes at its own location, does it measurably reshape the very nutrient gradient it depends on — niche construction (odling1996) — closing the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as real spatial metabolism: one cell sensing local glucose, running flux-balance analysis at its own footprint, growing, and secreting a byproduct that diffuses back into the field it senses? The paper frames this feedback loop as "the minimal structure required for control"; here the loop is genuinely closed (a real depletion → uptake feedback), while directed control — chemotaxis up the sensed gradient — is the deferred half. Mechanistically, the cell and its environment are two real, independently-built simulators (viva-cpm's Cellular Potts engine, spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes) composed through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField), rather than a scripted or lumped stand-in for that coupling.

Findings

  • mechanism A real CPM cell coupled to a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed process (CpmCellField) closes the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as genuine spatial metabolism (the loop is closed; directed control/chemotaxis is deferred): it runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint and grows both biologically (biomass) and structurally (CPM lattice volume). Confirmed: biomass 0.1 -> ~0.37, CPM volume 32 -> 110 px (~3.4x) over 20 ticks, on a 3600-pixel lattice the cell occupies only ~3% of by the end.
  • mechanism The cell measurably depletes the local nutrient it senses before diffusion partially resupplies it — niche construction as a direct spatial-metabolic consequence, not a scripted trajectory. Confirmed: local footprint glucose concentration peaks at 1.15 (t=2) and falls to 0.94 by t=20, an up-to-~18% local drawdown, while the field-wide glucose total falls by only ~32 of 5940 units — the depletion signature is sharply local to the cell's own footprint and its immediate diffusive neighborhood, not a field-wide event.
  • mechanism The O2-capped dynamic-FBA mechanism forces the optimizer into mixed-acid acetate overflow (rather than pure aerobic respiration), and that byproduct genuinely diffuses outward into a spreading plume via spatio-flux's own DiffusionAdvection process — the secretion side of the coupling is as real as the uptake side. Confirmed: field-wide acetate 0 -> ~47.0 over 20 ticks.
  • mechanism The acetate overflow is CAUSED BY the O2 cap, shown by experiment rather than asserted from code: lifting the oxygen bound (O2-uncapped control, oxygen_vmax 2.5 -> 1000) collapses acetate secretion to ~0 while the capped flagship secretes a real plume — the cell respires fully instead of overflowing (and grows faster for it). Confirmed: field-wide acetate capped 0 -> ~47.0 vs uncapped 0 -> 0.0 (exactly); uncapped biomass ~2.35 vs capped ~0.37.
  • mechanism INTERFACE SUBSTITUTABILITY — this investigation's demonstration of the paper's central claim. The paper (§2 / §Cellular interface) argues that "this substitutability is not only a software convenience. It allows the interface itself to become the object of comparison: different mechanisms may realize the same externally observable relation", and that "different internal organizations may sometimes be represented through the same coarse-grained interface when they preserve the interaction variables relevant to that description." Here that claim is run: the flagship cell's INTERNAL metabolism is swapped from constraint-based dynamic-FBA (cobra e_coli_core, O2-capped) to a lumped, cobra-free Michaelis–Menten / Monod metabolism (single-cell-in-a-field-mm, mechanism: mm) behind the BYTE-IDENTICAL CpmCellField ports, and the interface-level observables are compared head-to-head. Confirmed: over 20 ticks the two different internal organizations realize the same coarse-grained cell↔field interface — final biomass dFBA 0.370 vs MM 0.342 (7.5%), CPM volume 110 vs 99 px (10%), total acetate secreted 47.02 vs 47.02 (~0%), net glucose depleted 31.6 vs 33.6 units (6.2%); every interface observable agrees within ~10%. The MM twin additionally runs with NO cobra at all — the substitutability payoff, a mechanism that runs where the original could not. Stated honestly: the MM params (mm_vmax 4.0, mm_km 0.5, mm_yield 0.024, mm_overflow 1.4) were TUNED to preserve the interaction variables — the claim is "same coarse-grained relation", not identical internals, and not a derivation of MM from the dFBA solution.

Cell–Cell Coupling Accepted

Composing N real CPM cells with one shared, real spatio-flux nutrient field through a single N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduces the paper's §Cell–cell coupling viability-negotiation pattern (no dedicated figure) as genuine spatial multicellularity, not a scripted or lumped stand-in for it: over 20-tick runs, an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4) drives asymmetric growth toward competitive exclusion (biomass 237.9 vs 64.5, a 3.69x margin; volume 3511 vs 81 px — the loser displaced but still viable, above its 1.25 seed, not driven below a viability bound), while a different composite over the same coupling process — a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming role — sustains a cell that never touches glucose (local glucose 0.0) purely via a diffusing cross-feeding handoff (local acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79). Both regimes run through the same process; only the composite's cell roles and field constants differ, mirroring the lumped ODE predecessor's law-4 pattern (two handler environments negotiate the shared-resource constraint differently over one interface) at real spatial, per-footprint metabolic resolution.

Question & findings

Question

Does the paper's §Cell–cell coupling viability-negotiation pattern (no dedicated figure) hold as real spatial multicellularity — multiple CPM cells sharing one diffusing nutrient field, each running its own dynamic-FBA step at its own footprint, producing both competitive exclusion and cross-feeding — when the whole colony is composed from independent frameworks through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField), rather than the lumped two-role, shared-scalar-pool stand-in a lumped, specification-stage realization used?

Findings

  • mechanism Multiple real CPM cells sharing one real spatio-flux nutrient field through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduce the paper's §Cell–cell coupling shared-resource-competition branch (no dedicated figure) as genuine spatial multicellularity: an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4), with each cell running its own independent dFBA solve at its own footprint on the same depleting field, drives a decisive biomass and lattice-volume split -- asymmetric growth TOWARD competitive exclusion. Confirmed: biomass 237.9 vs 64.5 (3.69x), CPM volume 3511 vs 81 px, over a 20-tick run. (Strict exclusion is not reached -- the loser is displaced but stays viable; see F-04.)
  • mechanism The competition regime reaches asymmetric growth, NOT true competitive exclusion, over the run measured. The loser's biomass GREW from its 1.25 seed to 64.5 (~51.6x) -- far above the viable-biomass floor -- while shrinking to an 81-px fragment against the winner's 3511 px: it is spatially DISPLACED, not driven below any viability bound. This study couples STATE (one shared field) but imposes no viability CONSTRAINT (no death, no viability bound), exactly the paper's §Cell–cell coupling distinction that "what is coupled is not only state, but constraint" -- here, only state. The winner's 3511-px endpoint is ~97.5% of the 3600-px lattice with the field nearly exhausted by t=20, so the endpoint is partly a finite-size lattice-saturation artifact; by t≈30 the winner fills the lattice and the still-viable loser is crowded off it (eviction, not starvation).
  • mechanism Over the same coupling process, a different composite -- a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming cell role -- sustains a cell that never touches glucose purely via a spatial metabolic handoff: the consumer's local glucose reads 0.0 the entire run while its biomass still climbs, entirely on diffused acetate. This is the paper's §Cell–cell coupling relational-molecule claim made concrete -- the same acetate is the secretor's metabolic waste and the consumer's sole carbon food, its meaning set by each cell's interface (microbial cross-feeding, morris2013). Confirmed: consumer local_glucose 0.0, local_acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79, ending as a viable 65-pixel CPM cell.
  • mechanism The cross-feeding handoff is NECESSARY, not incidental: a secretor-knockout control (cellcell-crossfeed-knockout -- the cross-feed composite with the acetate secretor cell removed so no acetate is produced anywhere) leaves the otherwise-identical consumer unable to grow. It sits at local acetate 0.0 and its biomass never climbs off its 1.25 seed (persisting on the lattice at ~35 px), against the 1.25 -> 3.79 growth the same consumer shows when the secretor is present. This closes the handoff-necessity argument the flagship left resting only on local_glucose == 0.0 + observed growth: the consumer's growth is genuinely caused by the secretor's acetate, not by anything intrinsic to the consumer or the field.
  • mechanism The additive two-writer assumption CpmColonyField's N-cell field update depends on -- that disjoint per-cell footprint deltas sum losslessly into one shared field -- is runtime-verified directly and independently of any dFBA mechanism, retiring what was previously an unverified assumption in the flagship study. Confirmed structurally: two synthetic single-pixel writers on disjoint pixels each apply independently and their combined effect exactly matches the sum of the two, over repeated ticks.

Disintegration Accepted

Composing one real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux acetate field and a real spatio-flux particle-movement process through two coupling processes (CpmDisintegration for the viability-collapse trigger and resorption, stock BrownianMovement for the debris scatter) reproduces the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) as genuine spatial dissolution, not a scripted or lumped stand-in for it: over a fixed-seed deterministic 20-tick CPM run, the cell holds coherent (area > 30) through tick 6, the footprint-local acetate mean crosses viability_threshold at released_tick = 7, the domain resorbs to area 0 by tick 16, and its 68 shed pixels become a debris cloud whose RMS spread strictly increases through tick 24 (the trajectory -- hold, cross, resorb, particle count -- reproduces exactly; the scatter cloud moves under unseeded BrownianMovement, so its exact RMS magnitudes are representative of one run, not run-to-run-reproducible -- only the strict-increase direction is the robust claim) -- hold, cross, resorb, scatter, end to end from one coupling process plus one stock movement process, mirroring a lumped, specification-stage realization's §Disintegration level-shift pattern (Fig 6) at real spatial, per-pixel resolution.

Question & findings

Question

Does the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) hold spatially -- a coherent CPM cell that loses structural viability when a diffusing field of acetate (the SAME metabolic byproduct that is waste-and-food in cell-cell-coupling, here toxic at high local concentration) crosses its viability bound, its domain resorbing while its shed material becomes scattering physical particles -- composed from independent frameworks (viva-cpm + spatio-flux particles) through one coupling process (CpmDisintegration + BrownianMovement)?

Findings

  • mechanism A single real CPM cell coupled to a real spatio-flux acetate field through one coupling process (CpmDisintegration) reproduces the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) as genuine spatial dissolution: the cell holds coherent while the diffusing acetate field is below its footprint-local viability bound, then latches a release once that bound is crossed at a specific, deterministic tick, and its domain resorbs to full dissolution over the following ticks. Confirmed: area > 30 through tick 6, released_tick == 7.0, area == 0 by tick 16, all deterministic across repeated runs.
  • mechanism The cell's shed material does not vanish -- it becomes physical debris. As the domain resorbs, its vacated footprint pixels are converted into particles in a shared map[particle] store, and that debris cloud keeps scattering (RMS spread strictly increasing) well after the cell itself has fully dissolved, under a separate stock BrownianMovement process. Confirmed: 68 particles shed by tick 16 (deterministic, reproduces exactly); RMS spread ~5.3 -> ~7.6 over an 8-tick post-dissolution window in a representative run -- BrownianMovement is unseeded, so the exact magnitudes vary run-to-run, but the strict increase is the robust, test-asserted claim.
  • mechanism The CPM-to-particle bridge this study's disintegration mechanism depends on -- that a process can emit particles via the map _add sentinel into a shared map[particle] store, and a separate stock movement process can then move them -- is runtime-verified directly and independently of the disintegration mechanism itself, retiring what was previously an untested primitive. Confirmed structurally: a synthetic emitter's 3 particles remain exactly 3 particles four ticks later, with at least 2 having moved under BrownianMovement.

Molecular Interfaces Accepted

The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 7) holds spatially for two of its four physical channels. The CHEMICAL channel, realized as a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion system (local autocatalysis U + 2V -> 3V plus differential diffusion Dv < Du), turns a near-uniform seed into an emergent Turing spot pattern: v_var rises from ~0.0011 to ~0.0119 with ~12 connected domains (patterned == 1.0). The equal-diffusion causal control (Du = Dv = 0.12, chemistry unchanged -- the single differing variable) stays UNIFORM (v_var ~= 0, patterned == 0.0), so the pattern is CAUSED by the diffusion-driven instability, not the chemistry alone. The THERMAL channel -- a raised temperature field scaling the reaction by the Arrhenius factor exp(-Ea*(1/T - 1/Tref)) -- GRADES the pattern (Ea=0.15, T=1.2: ~12 domains -> ~2, v_var ~0.0119 -> ~0.0096) while staying patterned: a real second modality composing over the same field, not a collapse. Multi-seed (seeds 1-5, physics is deterministic so the seed is re-drawn into the initial field): v_var in [0.01157, 0.01193], n_domains in [8, 12] -- reported as a range. The honest boundaries are findings, not caveats: the ELECTROSTATIC and MECHANICAL channels are the named four-modality gap; the databases claim (PDB/Reactome/ChEBI/GO as partial specifications of molecular-interface types) is realized as an ILLUSTRATIVE typed-port annotation over the Gray-Scott species, not a resolved mapping; and this is a toy reaction-diffusion demonstration, not real molecular structure or conformation.

Question & findings

Question

The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 7) frames a molecule not as a lump of state but as a typed interface exposing several physical channels -- chemical, electrostatic, mechanical, thermal -- through which molecules bind, react, and transmit force and heat. It even names the public databases (PDB, Reactome, ChEBI, GO) as "partial specifications of molecular interfaces and their types". This study asks the discriminating question one channel down: does that molecular interface hold SPATIALLY? Concretely -- do local molecular reactions plus differential diffusion (the CHEMICAL channel spatialized, i.e. Turing / reaction-diffusion patterning) turn a near-uniform seed into emergent spatial structure, and does a temperature field GRADE that structure (the THERMAL channel, an Arrhenius rate modulation) -- so that two of the interface's physical channels demonstrably COMPOSE over one shared spatial field? The causal control -- equal diffusion (Du = Dv), chemistry untouched -- differs in exactly one variable and isolates the diffusion-driven instability as the cause of structure, so the contrast between pattern and uniformity is the finding.

Findings

  • mechanism The paper's §Molecular interface (Fig 7) CHEMICAL channel holds spatially: a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion system (local autocatalysis U + 2V -> 3V plus differential diffusion Dv < Du) turns a near-uniform seed into an emergent Turing spot pattern -- v_var rises ~0.0011 -> ~0.0119 and the field resolves into ~12 connected domains (patterned == 1.0). Local molecular reactions plus differential diffusion produce emergent spatial structure, the molecular interface's chemical channel made spatial.
  • mechanism The pattern is CAUSED by the diffusion-driven (Turing) instability, proven by a single-variable causal control. The equal-diffusion control (Du = Dv = 0.12 -- identical chemistry, seed, and wiring, the only difference the removal of the differential-diffusion asymmetry) stays UNIFORM: v_var ~= 0 (exactly 0.0), patterned == 0.0. Removing only the diffusion asymmetry removes the structure, isolating differential diffusion -- not the chemistry alone -- as its cause.
  • mechanism The THERMAL channel composes with the chemical one over the same field: a raised uniform temperature field (Ea=0.15, Tref=1.0, T=1.2) Arrhenius-scaling the reaction GRADES the pattern rather than destroying it -- coarsening it from ~12 domains to ~2 and lowering v_var ~0.0119 -> ~0.0096 while staying comfortably patterned (patterned == 1.0). A real second physical modality modulating the first. (A hotter Ea=1.0/T=1.5 setting overshoots into near-uniform collapse and was retuned; the overshoot is pinned as a direct-physics assertion, so "graded, not collapsed" is tested against its own failure mode.) Two of the molecular interface's four channels demonstrably compose spatially.
  • observation The honest scope, load-bearing not buried -- the FOUR-MODALITY finding. This study realizes TWO of the paper's four molecular-interface channels spatially (CHEMICAL: reaction-diffusion / Turing; THERMAL: Arrhenius rate grading). The ELECTROSTATIC and MECHANICAL channels are the named gap -- no charge field, no force or tension, is exposed or measured (owned in investigation.yaml as a named deferral). Further: this is a TOY reaction-diffusion demonstration with tuned, uncalibrated constants -- emergent structure from a minimal autocatalytic chemistry, NOT real molecular structure or conformation (nothing at PDB resolution). And the databases claim (PDB/Reactome/ChEBI/GO as partial specifications of molecular-interface types) is realized as an ILLUSTRATIVE typed-port annotation over the Gray-Scott species, NOT a resolved database mapping; a real Reactome-fragment mapping / live database fetch is a deferral.
  • observation DETERMINISM, stated plainly. The Gray-Scott physics has NO RNG -- the GrayScott seed config key is accepted but currently inert -- so the multi-seed check re-draws the INITIAL near-uniform field with seed_uv(seed=s) for s in 1-5 (everything else fixed) rather than varying a per-tick stochastic process. The pattern forms for every seed; v_var in [0.01157, 0.01193] and n_domains in [8, 12] are reported as a RANGE reflecting genuine sensitivity of the emergent pattern to nucleation-patch placement, not dressed as a single golden value.

Biomolecular Complementarity Accepted

Differential-adhesion cell sorting reproduces the paper's §Molecular compositions complementarity (Fig 8) as a spatial mechanism: two cell types differing only in which contacts they find energetically favorable demix a well-mixed checkerboard (heterotypic-interface fraction ~0.64 -> ~0.06 over ~600 Monte Carlo steps) while staying cohesive (live-cell pixels ~1530, <1% drift; per-type counts constant at 32/32) -- like sitting with like is the spatial reading of which interface variables must align for coupling. Independently, a Cahn-Hilliard composition field realizes the condensate mechanism the paper draws from banani2017: a near-critical field phase-separates into two domains bounded by a sharp, emergent interface (variance ~7e-5 -> ~0.38 over 10000 steps) with mass conserved to ~4e-18 and the field bounded in [-1, 1]. The honest boundary of the claim is explicit: the condensate forms an interface but does not yet do interface work -- it does not selectively concentrate or exclude a second species -- so it demonstrates a boundary forming, not yet the functional, regulatable boundary the paper's central question distinguishes from a transient aggregate. Neither regime computes a binding affinity; both are tuned demonstration primitives, not fitted to a measured tissue or condensate.
