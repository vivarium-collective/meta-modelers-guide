Introduction

The publication figures for the meta-modeler's guide, upgraded from a figure bundle into a full evaluative investigation. Each figure keeps its BioRender illustration (panel a) and its bigraph-loom diagram (panel b) exactly as published, and now carries the executable substance behind that diagram: a runnable figNN-runnable process-bigraph composite plus a behavior-gated principle test that demonstrates the figure's caption on genuine simulation output. The organizing claim is that the paper's eleven figures are executable — every panel-b diagram denotes a real composition that runs and reproduces its stated principle, not a hand-drawn schematic. The figures remain downloadable as a bundle under final/; the investigation adds the runnable models, tests, and per-figure verdicts on top of them.

These are DIDACTIC, TOY-REAL compositions: small, illustrative, built to resemble the figure and play forward, with uncalibrated constants. That is deliberate — the figures are teaching artifacts, and their job is to make the paper's principle legible and playable, not to fit literature. Read this investigation as "the paper's figures execute their captions," and draft-to-living-cell as "the paper's patterns hold under calibrated spatial rigor."
