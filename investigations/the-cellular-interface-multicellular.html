The Cellular Interface, Multicellular

This is the spatial, multicellular-capable counterpart to draft-to-living-cell: the same nine interface-composition patterns from the paper, realized this time as real 2D spatial frameworks — chiefly a Cellular Potts Model (viva-cpm) coupled to spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes — rather than lumped ODEs and scripted events. Where draft-to-living-cell asked "can this pattern be authored as an inert typed draft and compiled into a runnable mechanism," this investigation asks "does the pattern survive when the mechanism is a real spatial simulator built by someone else, composed in through one typed interface." The flagship, cell-environment-coupling-spatial, builds and runs exactly one such composition: CpmCellField, a process that reads a spatio-flux nutrient grid at a CPM cell's own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step there (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows the cell's CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes the uptake/secretion back into the shared field as a spatial delta that spatio-flux's DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks the cell grows roughly 3.4x (32 to 110 CPM lattice pixels, on a 60x60 = 3600-pixel field — it does not come close to filling the lattice), depletes the glucose at its own footprint by up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and secretes a spreading acetate plume — niche construction made spatially and metabolically real, not scripted. Three more studies are built and run alongside the flagship — cell-cell coupling (multi-cell field competition), disintegration (structural-integrity release), and growth-and-division. The remaining five (the cellular-interface contract itself, molecular-scale reaction-diffusion, sorting and phase separation, a self-maintained protocell, and colony development) are planned or in progress, not yet built; this file is honest about that.

What this investigation has NOT yet shown is the remaining patterns' spatial counterparts — reaction-diffusion molecular mechanisms, differential-adhesion sorting and phase separation, a self-maintained protocell, colony development, and the cellular-interface contract itself. Those are named in the design spec (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-21-cellular-interface-multicellular-design.md) as the increment plan; some are planned, others in progress. Chemotaxis toward the field (the cell moving up the gradient it senses) is also deferred to a follow-up variant — the flagship cell's modest position drift over the run is CPM thermal fluctuation, not directed motion.

Introduction

This investigation is the spatial sibling of draft-to-living-cell. That investigation turned the cellular-interface paper's nine composition patterns into inert typed drafts compiled into small, lumped executables (ODE-scale mechanisms on 1-D or 9-cell grids). This investigation asks the harder question: do those same patterns still compose when the frameworks on each side of the interface are real, independently-built 2D spatial simulators — a Cellular Potts Model (viva-cpm, a Rust-backed lattice engine with genuine cell shape, adhesion, and volume dynamics) on one side, and spatio-flux's diffusion-advection PDE solver plus dynamic flux-balance analysis on the other? Composing two frameworks that were never designed with each other in mind is exactly the paper's central claim about typed interfaces made concrete: CpmCellField, the flagship's coupling process, is the interface where a CPM cell's footprint (a set of lattice pixels) is translated into a spatio-flux field read, and where a dynamic-FBA solution (mmol/gDW/hr fluxes) is translated back into a per-pixel field delta and a CPM target-volume update. Four studies are built and run so far — cell-environment-coupling-spatial (the flagship, the spatial analogue of Fig 5's sense/act loop), cell-cell-coupling-spatial, disintegration-spatial, and growth-and-division-spatial. The remaining patterns from draft-to-living-cell (molecular interfaces, biomolecular complementarity, autopoiesis, development-and-evolution, and the cellular-interface contract itself) are named as future increments in the design spec; some are planned, one (biomolecular complementarity) is in progress with code done but its study not yet accepted.

The investigations are deliberate analogues, study-for-study, wherever the counterpart exists: cell-environment-coupling-spatial here is the spatial realization of draft-to-living-cell/cell-environment-coupling. Read them side by side — the lumped 9-cell 1-D executable there against the real 60x60 2D CPM+spatio-flux composite here — for the same interface claim (sensing and acting are one coupling, not two subsystems; the cell measurably reshapes the gradient it depends on) demonstrated at two very different levels of mechanistic resolution.

Evidence for

  • cell-environment-coupling-spatial (the spatial Fig 5, this investigation's flagship) — a real CPM cell reads a spatio-flux glucose field at its own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows its CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes uptake/secretion back into the field as a spatial delta that DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks: CPM volume grows ~3.4x (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of the 60x60 lattice — the cell does not fill it), local footprint glucose depletes up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and the field-wide acetate total rises from 0 to ~47 (mmol-equivalent field units) as the secreted plume diffuses outward. Net field-wide glucose falls (~32 of 5940 total field mass consumed) even though the depletion signal is much sharper locally at the cell's own footprint than in the field-wide total — the direct niche-construction signature.

Evidence against

  • Five of the nine draft-to-living-cell patterns (molecular interfaces, biomolecular complementarity, autopoiesis, development-and-evolution, and the cellular-interface contract itself) have no spatial counterpart built and run yet — this investigation currently demonstrates four patterns, not nine.
  • Chemotaxis (directed up-gradient motion) is deferred; the flagship cell senses and acts on the field locally but does not move toward higher concentration.
  • The e_coli_core constants (glucose_km, glucose_vmax, oxygen_vmax, box_volume_L, grow_per_biomass) are toy-real — plausible, hand-tuned magnitudes chosen to produce gradual, spatially-legible dynamics over a short run, not fitted to any organism or medium.
  • The CPM lattice itself (the cell's shape, occupied pixels, footprint) lives inside viva-cpm's Rust engine as a private, write-protected world object (CpmCellField.world), not a process-bigraph store — only the coupling process reads and writes it. The "environment" the interface exposes to the rest of the composite is exclusively the spatio-flux fields store (glucose, acetate); the CPM's own internal field concept (field_at_cell, secretory_types) is separate machinery this composite does not use.

Cell–Environment Coupling, Spatial (flagship) Accepted

Composing a real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField) reproduces the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as genuine spatial metabolism, not a scripted stand-in for it (the loop is closed; directed control/chemotaxis is deferred): over 20 ticks the cell runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint (e_coli_core, O2-capped to force acetate overflow), grows ~3.4x in CPM volume (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of a 60x60 lattice it does not come close to filling), depletes local glucose by up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it (niche construction), and secretes a diffusing acetate plume — two independently-built simulators talking through one typed port.

Question & findings

Question

When a single cell metabolizes at its own location, does it measurably reshape the very nutrient gradient it depends on — niche construction (odling1996) — closing the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as real spatial metabolism: one cell sensing local glucose, running flux-balance analysis at its own footprint, growing, and secreting a byproduct that diffuses back into the field it senses? The paper frames this feedback loop as "the minimal structure required for control"; here the loop is genuinely closed (a real depletion → uptake feedback), while directed control — chemotaxis up the sensed gradient — is the deferred half. Mechanistically, the cell and its environment are two real, independently-built simulators (viva-cpm's Cellular Potts engine, spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes) composed through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField), rather than a scripted or lumped stand-in for that coupling.

Findings

  • mechanism A real CPM cell coupled to a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed process (CpmCellField) closes the paper's §Cell–environment coupling sense → metabolize → secrete loop (Fig 5) as genuine spatial metabolism (the loop is closed; directed control/chemotaxis is deferred): it runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint and grows both biologically (biomass) and structurally (CPM lattice volume). Confirmed: biomass 0.1 -> ~0.37, CPM volume 32 -> 110 px (~3.4x) over 20 ticks, on a 3600-pixel lattice the cell occupies only ~3% of by the end.
  • mechanism The cell measurably depletes the local nutrient it senses before diffusion partially resupplies it — niche construction as a direct spatial-metabolic consequence, not a scripted trajectory. Confirmed: local footprint glucose concentration peaks at 1.15 (t=2) and falls to 0.94 by t=20, an up-to-~18% local drawdown, while the field-wide glucose total falls by only ~32 of 5940 units — the depletion signature is sharply local to the cell's own footprint and its immediate diffusive neighborhood, not a field-wide event.
  • mechanism The O2-capped dynamic-FBA mechanism forces the optimizer into mixed-acid acetate overflow (rather than pure aerobic respiration), and that byproduct genuinely diffuses outward into a spreading plume via spatio-flux's own DiffusionAdvection process — the secretion side of the coupling is as real as the uptake side. Confirmed: field-wide acetate 0 -> ~47.0 over 20 ticks.
  • mechanism The acetate overflow is CAUSED BY the O2 cap, shown by experiment rather than asserted from code: lifting the oxygen bound (O2-uncapped control, oxygen_vmax 2.5 -> 1000) collapses acetate secretion to ~0 while the capped flagship secretes a real plume — the cell respires fully instead of overflowing (and grows faster for it). Confirmed: field-wide acetate capped 0 -> ~47.0 vs uncapped 0 -> 0.0 (exactly); uncapped biomass ~2.35 vs capped ~0.37.
  • mechanism INTERFACE SUBSTITUTABILITY — this investigation's demonstration of the paper's central claim. The paper (§2 / §Cellular interface) argues that "this substitutability is not only a software convenience. It allows the interface itself to become the object of comparison: different mechanisms may realize the same externally observable relation", and that "different internal organizations may sometimes be represented through the same coarse-grained interface when they preserve the interaction variables relevant to that description." Here that claim is run: the flagship cell's INTERNAL metabolism is swapped from constraint-based dynamic-FBA (cobra e_coli_core, O2-capped) to a lumped, cobra-free Michaelis–Menten / Monod metabolism (single-cell-in-a-field-mm, mechanism: mm) behind the BYTE-IDENTICAL CpmCellField ports, and the interface-level observables are compared head-to-head. Confirmed: over 20 ticks the two different internal organizations realize the same coarse-grained cell↔field interface — final biomass dFBA 0.370 vs MM 0.342 (7.5%), CPM volume 110 vs 99 px (10%), total acetate secreted 47.02 vs 47.02 (~0%), net glucose depleted 31.6 vs 33.6 units (6.2%); every interface observable agrees within ~10%. The MM twin additionally runs with NO cobra at all — the substitutability payoff, a mechanism that runs where the original could not. Stated honestly: the MM params (mm_vmax 4.0, mm_km 0.5, mm_yield 0.024, mm_overflow 1.4) were TUNED to preserve the interaction variables — the claim is "same coarse-grained relation", not identical internals, and not a derivation of MM from the dFBA solution.

Cell–Cell Coupling, Spatial Accepted

Composing N real CPM cells with one shared, real spatio-flux nutrient field through a single N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduces the paper's §Cell–cell coupling viability-negotiation pattern (no dedicated figure) as genuine spatial multicellularity, not a scripted or lumped stand-in for it: over 20-tick runs, an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4) drives asymmetric growth toward competitive exclusion (biomass 237.9 vs 64.5, a 3.69x margin; volume 3511 vs 81 px — the loser displaced but still viable, above its 1.25 seed, not driven below a viability bound), while a different composite over the same coupling process — a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming role — sustains a cell that never touches glucose (local glucose 0.0) purely via a diffusing cross-feeding handoff (local acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79). Both regimes run through the same process; only the composite's cell roles and field constants differ, mirroring draft-to-living-cell/cell-cell-coupling's law-4 pattern (two handler environments negotiate the shared-resource constraint differently over one interface) at real spatial, per-footprint metabolic resolution.

Question & findings

Question

Does the paper's §Cell–cell coupling viability-negotiation pattern (no dedicated figure) hold as real spatial multicellularity — multiple CPM cells sharing one diffusing nutrient field, each running its own dynamic-FBA step at its own footprint, producing both competitive exclusion and cross-feeding — when the whole colony is composed from independent frameworks through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField), rather than the lumped two-role, shared-scalar-pool stand-in draft-to-living-cell's §Cell–cell coupling study used?

Findings

  • mechanism Multiple real CPM cells sharing one real spatio-flux nutrient field through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduce the paper's §Cell–cell coupling shared-resource-competition branch (no dedicated figure) as genuine spatial multicellularity: an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4), with each cell running its own independent dFBA solve at its own footprint on the same depleting field, drives a decisive biomass and lattice-volume split -- asymmetric growth TOWARD competitive exclusion. Confirmed: biomass 237.9 vs 64.5 (3.69x), CPM volume 3511 vs 81 px, over a 20-tick run. (Strict exclusion is not reached -- the loser is displaced but stays viable; see F-04.)
  • mechanism The competition regime reaches asymmetric growth, NOT true competitive exclusion, over the run measured. The loser's biomass GREW from its 1.25 seed to 64.5 (~51.6x) -- far above the viable-biomass floor -- while shrinking to an 81-px fragment against the winner's 3511 px: it is spatially DISPLACED, not driven below any viability bound. This study couples STATE (one shared field) but imposes no viability CONSTRAINT (no death, no viability bound), exactly the paper's §Cell–cell coupling distinction that "what is coupled is not only state, but constraint" -- here, only state. The winner's 3511-px endpoint is ~97.5% of the 3600-px lattice with the field nearly exhausted by t=20, so the endpoint is partly a finite-size lattice-saturation artifact; by t≈30 the winner fills the lattice and the still-viable loser is crowded off it (eviction, not starvation).
  • mechanism Over the same coupling process, a different composite -- a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming cell role -- sustains a cell that never touches glucose purely via a spatial metabolic handoff: the consumer's local glucose reads 0.0 the entire run while its biomass still climbs, entirely on diffused acetate. This is the paper's §Cell–cell coupling relational-molecule claim made concrete -- the same acetate is the secretor's metabolic waste and the consumer's sole carbon food, its meaning set by each cell's interface (microbial cross-feeding, morris2013). Confirmed: consumer local_glucose 0.0, local_acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79, ending as a viable 65-pixel CPM cell.
  • mechanism The cross-feeding handoff is NECESSARY, not incidental: a secretor-knockout control (cellcell-crossfeed-knockout -- the cross-feed composite with the acetate secretor cell removed so no acetate is produced anywhere) leaves the otherwise-identical consumer unable to grow. It sits at local acetate 0.0 and its biomass never climbs off its 1.25 seed (persisting on the lattice at ~35 px), against the 1.25 -> 3.79 growth the same consumer shows when the secretor is present. This closes the handoff-necessity argument the flagship left resting only on local_glucose == 0.0 + observed growth: the consumer's growth is genuinely caused by the secretor's acetate, not by anything intrinsic to the consumer or the field.
  • mechanism The additive two-writer assumption CpmColonyField's N-cell field update depends on -- that disjoint per-cell footprint deltas sum losslessly into one shared field -- is runtime-verified directly and independently of any dFBA mechanism, retiring what was previously an unverified assumption in the flagship study. Confirmed structurally: two synthetic single-pixel writers on disjoint pixels each apply independently and their combined effect exactly matches the sum of the two, over repeated ticks.

Disintegration, Spatial Accepted

Composing one real CPM cell with a real spatio-flux acetate field and a real spatio-flux particle-movement process through two coupling processes (CpmDisintegration for the viability-collapse trigger and resorption, stock BrownianMovement for the debris scatter) reproduces the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) as genuine spatial dissolution, not a scripted or lumped stand-in for it: over a fixed-seed deterministic 20-tick CPM run, the cell holds coherent (area > 30) through tick 6, the footprint-local acetate mean crosses viability_threshold at released_tick = 7, the domain resorbs to area 0 by tick 16, and its 68 shed pixels become a debris cloud whose RMS spread strictly increases through tick 24 (the trajectory -- hold, cross, resorb, particle count -- reproduces exactly; the scatter cloud moves under unseeded BrownianMovement, so its exact RMS magnitudes are representative of one run, not run-to-run-reproducible -- only the strict-increase direction is the robust claim) -- hold, cross, resorb, scatter, end to end from one coupling process plus one stock movement process, mirroring draft-to-living-cell/disintegration's §Disintegration level-shift pattern (Fig 6) at real spatial, per-pixel resolution.

Question & findings

Question

Does the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) hold spatially -- a coherent CPM cell that loses structural viability when a diffusing field of acetate (the SAME metabolic byproduct that is waste-and-food in cell-cell-coupling-spatial, here toxic at high local concentration) crosses its viability bound, its domain resorbing while its shed material becomes scattering physical particles -- composed from independent frameworks (viva-cpm + spatio-flux particles) through one coupling process (CpmDisintegration + BrownianMovement)?

Findings

  • mechanism A single real CPM cell coupled to a real spatio-flux acetate field through one coupling process (CpmDisintegration) reproduces the paper's §Disintegration level-shift (Fig 6) as genuine spatial dissolution: the cell holds coherent while the diffusing acetate field is below its footprint-local viability bound, then latches a release once that bound is crossed at a specific, deterministic tick, and its domain resorbs to full dissolution over the following ticks. Confirmed: area > 30 through tick 6, released_tick == 7.0, area == 0 by tick 16, all deterministic across repeated runs.
  • mechanism The cell's shed material does not vanish -- it becomes physical debris. As the domain resorbs, its vacated footprint pixels are converted into particles in a shared map[particle] store, and that debris cloud keeps scattering (RMS spread strictly increasing) well after the cell itself has fully dissolved, under a separate stock BrownianMovement process. Confirmed: 68 particles shed by tick 16 (deterministic, reproduces exactly); RMS spread ~5.3 -> ~7.6 over an 8-tick post-dissolution window in a representative run -- BrownianMovement is unseeded, so the exact magnitudes vary run-to-run, but the strict increase is the robust, test-asserted claim.
  • mechanism The CPM-to-particle bridge this study's disintegration mechanism depends on -- that a process can emit particles via the map _add sentinel into a shared map[particle] store, and a separate stock movement process can then move them -- is runtime-verified directly and independently of the disintegration mechanism itself, retiring what was previously an untested primitive. Confirmed structurally: a synthetic emitter's 3 particles remain exactly 3 particles four ticks later, with at least 2 having moved under BrownianMovement.

Growth and Division, Spatial Accepted

Metabolism-driven growth crossing a threshold fires division, and division partitions state -- biomass, and lattice pixels -- across two daughters that resume growing themselves, compounding into a genuine multi-generation lineage tree whose siblings desynchronize as they compete for shared, local resources: this is the paper's §Growth and division growth-then-divide pattern (Fig 10a,b), including its "coordination and divergence" clause, realized spatially. One real CPM cell, growing via real per-footprint dFBA metabolism, divides via the native CPM engine operation divide_cells (biomass partitioned proportional to post-split daughter volume, not reset and discarded) through a single coupling process (CpmGrowthDivision). Over a 36-tick run sampled every 3 ticks: the founder compounds into an 18-cell lineage tree spanning 4 generations (max_generation = 4), the population steps up in a monotonic non-decreasing staircase (1,2,2,2,4,4,5,8,8,11,14,18) that departs from clean powers of two from tick 21 onward as sibling cells' division timing diverges, and every cell's CPM volume stays bounded in a sawtooth between reset_target = 40 and vol_threshold = 80. The staircase is bounded more by lattice crowding (the colony packs to roughly two-thirds occupancy within its own footprint) than by nutrient limitation (field-wide glucose depletes only ~3% over the run). This is direct simulation output from three independently-developed frameworks (viva-cpm + spatio-flux + cobra) meeting at one typed interface. It mirrors draft-to-living-cell/growth-and-division's §Growth and division claim (Fig 10a,b) (growth drives the cell's own state across a threshold; crossing the threshold triggers division) but realizes division as the native spatial CPM operation divide_cells acting directly on lattice pixels, in contrast to that study's place-graph rewrite (one node becoming two structurally-new nodes at a lumped, non-spatial level of composition).

Question & findings

Question

A cell grows, crosses a threshold, and divides -- and its daughters do not necessarily divide together. This is the paper's §Growth and division growth-then-divide pattern (Fig 10a,b): metabolism drives a cell's own state across a threshold, crossing it fires division and partitions state across the two resulting daughters, and those daughters, "remain[ing] coupled through shared environmental state," can either stay in step or diverge (§Growth and division). Does this hold spatially -- a single CPM cell whose metabolism (dFBA solved at its own lattice footprint) grows its volume until it crosses vol_threshold and divides via the native CPM engine operation divide_cells, partitioning biomass mass-conserved across the two daughters, compounding into a small multi-generation lineage tree -- and do sibling cells sharing one glucose field begin growing at different local rates as the colony crowds, so their division timing desynchronizes rather than staying locked to clean powers of two? Composed from independent frameworks (viva-cpm + spatio-flux + cobra) through one coupling process (CpmGrowthDivision).

Findings

  • mechanism A single real CPM cell whose volume growth is driven by real per-footprint dFBA metabolism, coupled to the native CPM engine operation divide_cells through one process (CpmGrowthDivision), reproduces the paper's §Growth and division growth-then-divide pattern (Fig 10a,b) as genuine spatial compounding: the population steps up in a monotonic non-decreasing staircase, reaching roughly 3-4 generations from a single seed cell, bounded more by lattice crowding than by nutrient limitation (field-wide glucose depletes only ~3% over the run). Confirmed: n_cells trajectory 1,2,2,2,4,4,5,8,8,11,14,18 over 36 ticks.
  • mechanism Every cell in the lineage sawtooths in a bounded CPM volume band as it grows toward vol_threshold, is halved by division back near reset_target, and immediately resumes growing -- daughters are never zero-volume phantoms and no single cell runs away to absorb the lattice. Confirmed: per-cell volume stays roughly 31-79 px throughout the run, well within the hard test bounds of (8, 200).
  • mechanism The native CPM divide_cells operation this study's division mechanism depends on is runtime-verified directly and independently of the growth-division composite's own metabolism mechanism, retiring what was previously an untested primitive: a grown cell splits into two mass-conserved daughters (parent id kept, one new id minted) and a sub-threshold cell is a correct no-op.
  • mechanism Sibling cells coupled through the shared glucose field grow at different local rates as the colony crowds -- a cell on the fed rim out-competes one boxed into a depleting interior -- so division timing desynchronizes rather than staying locked to clean powers of two. This is exactly the paper's own description of what happens right after division: "these systems remain coupled through shared environmental state, so their interfaces are not independent ... enabling both coordination and divergence" (§Growth and division). It is the first emergent multicellular heterogeneity this investigation demonstrates, not a nuisance the staircase merely survives. Confirmed: the n_cells staircase departs from powers of two from tick 21 onward (5, then 11, 14, 18), and by the final sampled tick, live cells span all 5 generations (0 through 4) simultaneously in the same 18-cell colony.
  • mechanism The population count is a genealogy, not a tally. Per §Growth and division's Fig 10a,b "daughter systems ... inherit" language, every live cell's generation and parent id are tracked directly from the engine's own divide_cells return value (Phase 1's lineage/generation bookkeeping, never scripted): founder id 1 stays generation 0 for the whole run; its descendants populate generations 1 through 4, and every daughter's generation equals its recorded parent's generation plus one. Confirmed: max_generation compounds from 0 to 4 over the 36-tick run, and the full 18-cell tree traces back to the single founder.

Biomolecular Complementarity, Spatial Accepted

Differential-adhesion cell sorting reproduces the paper's §Molecular compositions complementarity (Fig 8) as a spatial mechanism: two cell types differing only in which contacts they find energetically favorable demix a well-mixed checkerboard (heterotypic-interface fraction ~0.64 -> ~0.06 over ~600 Monte Carlo steps) while staying cohesive (live-cell pixels ~1530, <1% drift; per-type counts constant at 32/32) -- like sitting with like is the spatial reading of which interface variables must align for coupling. Independently, a Cahn-Hilliard composition field realizes the condensate mechanism the paper draws from banani2017: a near-critical field phase-separates into two domains bounded by a sharp, emergent interface (variance ~7e-5 -> ~0.38 over 10000 steps) with mass conserved to ~4e-18 and the field bounded in [-1, 1]. The honest boundary of the claim is explicit: the condensate forms an interface but does not yet do interface work -- it does not selectively concentrate or exclude a second species -- so it demonstrates a boundary forming, not yet the functional, regulatable boundary the paper's central question distinguishes from a transient aggregate. Neither regime computes a binding affinity; both are tuned demonstration primitives, not fitted to a measured tissue or condensate. The two run as independent processes through the process-bigraph engine, with no coupling between them.

Question & findings

Question

The paper's §Molecular compositions section (Fig 8) frames its subject as a question about interfaces, not architecture: which patterns of molecular complementarity give rise to interfaces that behave as functional, regulatable boundaries, and which produce transient or unregulated aggregates? This study puts two spatial primitives of that question on the lattice. Differential-adhesion cell sorting -- Steinberg's Differential Adhesion Hypothesis -- is complementarity made spatial: a mixed population of two cell types, distinguished only by which contacts they find energetically favorable, demixes so that like sits with like and the heterotypic boundary between them collapses, a direct spatial reading of which interface variables must align for two cells to stay coupled. Biomolecular-condensate phase separation is the diffuse-interface counterpart: a scalar composition field, seeded near-critical, spontaneously separates into two phases divided by a sharp interface -- a boundary forming out of a well-mixed medium, the way a condensate droplet does. Each is a real 2D spatial mechanism run through the process-bigraph engine; the two are independent demonstrations, not one coupled model.

Findings

  • mechanism Differential-adhesion cell sorting reproduces the paper's §Molecular compositions complementarity (Fig 8) as a spatial mechanism: two cell types differing only in which contacts they find energetically favorable demix a well-mixed checkerboard so that like sits with like -- the spatial reading of which interface variables must align for coupling. Confirmed: heterotypic-interface fraction ~0.64 -> ~0.06 over ~600 MCS, while the clump stays cohesive (live-cell pixels ~1530, <1% drift) and per-type counts hold at 32/32.
  • mechanism A Cahn-Hilliard composition field realizes the condensate mechanism the paper draws from banani2017 (§Molecular compositions, Fig 8): weak-interaction-driven phase separation into droplets with an emergent interface. Confirmed as spinodal decomposition: a near-critical field separates into two phases (variance ~7e-5 -> ~0.38 over 10000 steps) bounded by a sharp interface, with total mass conserved to ~4e-18 and the field bounded in [-1, 1]. A boundary forms out of a well-mixed medium, mass-conserved.
  • observation Honesty point, load-bearing for the paper's central question. The condensate study demonstrates a boundary forming -- phase separation, mass-conserved, with an emergent interface -- but NOT the condensate doing interface work: it does not selectively concentrate or exclude a second species. That selective-permeability property is exactly what would make the interface a functional, regulatable boundary rather than a transient or unregulated aggregate, which is the discriminator the paper's §Molecular compositions question (Fig 8) turns on. It is named here as the natural next step, not claimed as already shown. Likewise the sorting study demonstrates differential-adhesion demixing with a cohesion guard, not a molecular binding-affinity/selectivity term.
  • mechanism The demixing is caused by the differential-J regime, not by CPM dynamics per se. Under neutral contact energies (all J=8) the same checkerboard at T=10 stays mixed, isolating the complementarity-encoding contact-energy asymmetry as the cause of sorting. Confirmed by a neutral-J composite re-run (documented, not a committed test): neutral J holds hetero_frac ~0.52 over 600 MCS vs ~0.06 for the differential-J regime.
