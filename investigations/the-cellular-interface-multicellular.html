The Cellular Interface, Multicellular

This is the spatial, multicellular-capable counterpart to draft-to-living-cell: the same nine interface-composition patterns from the paper, realized this time as real 2D spatial frameworks — chiefly a Cellular Potts Model (viva-cpm) coupled to spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes — rather than lumped ODEs and scripted events. Where draft-to-living-cell asked "can this pattern be authored as an inert typed draft and compiled into a runnable mechanism," this investigation asks "does the pattern survive when the mechanism is a real spatial simulator built by someone else, composed in through one typed interface." The flagship, cell-environment-coupling-spatial, builds and runs exactly one such composition: CpmCellField, a process that reads a spatio-flux nutrient grid at a CPM cell's own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step there (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows the cell's CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes the uptake/secretion back into the shared field as a spatial delta that spatio-flux's DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks the cell grows roughly 3.4x (32 to 110 CPM lattice pixels, on a 60x60 = 3600-pixel field — it does not come close to filling the lattice), depletes the glucose at its own footprint by up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and secretes a spreading acetate plume — niche construction made spatially and metabolically real, not scripted. The remaining eight studies (a multi-cell competition counterpart, a structural-integrity release, molecular-scale reaction-diffusion, sorting and phase separation, a self-maintained protocell, growth-and-division, and colony development) are planned, not built; this file and the flagship study are honest about that.

Chemotaxis toward the field (the cell moving up the gradient it senses) is also deferred to a follow-up variant — the flagship cell's modest position drift over the run is CPM thermal fluctuation, not directed motion.

Introduction

This investigation is the spatial sibling of draft-to-living-cell. That investigation turned the cellular-interface paper's nine composition patterns into inert typed drafts compiled into small, lumped executables (ODE-scale mechanisms on 1-D or 9-cell grids). This investigation asks the harder question: do those same patterns still compose when the frameworks on each side of the interface are real, independently-built 2D spatial simulators — a Cellular Potts Model (viva-cpm, a Rust-backed lattice engine with genuine cell shape, adhesion, and volume dynamics) on one side, and spatio-flux's diffusion-advection PDE solver plus dynamic flux-balance analysis on the other? Composing two frameworks that were never designed with each other in mind is exactly the paper's central claim about typed interfaces made concrete: CpmCellField, the one coupling process this investigation has built so far, is the interface where a CPM cell's footprint (a set of lattice pixels) is translated into a spatio-flux field read, and where a dynamic-FBA solution (mmol/gDW/hr fluxes) is translated back into a per-pixel field delta and a CPM target-volume update. Only the flagship study, cell-environment-coupling-spatial (the spatial analogue of Fig 5's sense/act loop), is built and run; the other eight patterns from draft-to-living-cell (cell-cell coupling, disintegration, molecular interfaces, biomolecular complementarity, autopoiesis, growth-and-division, development-and-evolution, and the cellular-interface contract itself) are named as future increments in the design spec but have no composite, study, or run yet.

The investigations are deliberate analogues, study-for-study, wherever the counterpart exists: cell-environment-coupling-spatial here is the spatial realization of draft-to-living-cell/cell-environment-coupling. Read them side by side — the lumped 9-cell 1-D executable there against the real 60x60 2D CPM+spatio-flux composite here — for the same interface claim (sensing and acting are one coupling, not two subsystems; the cell measurably reshapes the gradient it depends on) demonstrated at two very different levels of mechanistic resolution.

Evidence for

  • cell-environment-coupling-spatial (the spatial Fig 5, this investigation's flagship) — a real CPM cell reads a spatio-flux glucose field at its own footprint, runs one dynamic-FBA step (e_coli_core, oxygen-capped to force acetate overflow), grows its CPM target volume from the biomass produced, and writes uptake/secretion back into the field as a spatial delta that DiffusionAdvection then spreads. Over 20 ticks: CPM volume grows ~3.4x (32 -> 110 px, ~3% of the 60x60 lattice — the cell does not fill it), local footprint glucose depletes up to ~18% before diffusion partially resupplies it, and the field-wide acetate total rises from 0 to ~47 (mmol-equivalent field units) as the secreted plume diffuses outward. Net field-wide glucose falls (~32 of 5940 total field mass consumed) even though the depletion signal is much sharper locally at the cell's own footprint than in the field-wide total — the direct niche-construction signature.

Evidence against

  • The other eight draft-to-living-cell patterns have no spatial counterpart built yet — this investigation currently demonstrates one pattern (cell-environment coupling), not nine.
  • Chemotaxis (directed up-gradient motion) is deferred; the flagship cell senses and acts on the field locally but does not move toward higher concentration.
  • The e_coli_core constants (glucose_km, glucose_vmax, oxygen_vmax, box_volume_L, grow_per_biomass) are toy-real — plausible, hand-tuned magnitudes chosen to produce gradual, spatially-legible dynamics over a short run, not fitted to any organism or medium.
  • The CPM lattice itself (the cell's shape, occupied pixels, footprint) lives inside viva-cpm's Rust engine as a private, write-protected world object (CpmCellField.world), not a process-bigraph store — only the coupling process reads and writes it. The "environment" the interface exposes to the rest of the composite is exclusively the spatio-flux fields store (glucose, acetate); the CPM's own internal field concept (field_at_cell, secretory_types) is separate machinery this composite does not use.

Question & findings

Question

Does the Fig 5 sense/act loop hold as real spatial metabolism — one CPM cell sensing a diffusing nutrient field, running flux-balance analysis at its own footprint, growing, and secreting a byproduct back into the field — when the cell and its environment are each real, independently-built simulators (viva-cpm's Cellular Potts engine, spatio-flux's diffusion-advection and dynamic-FBA processes) composed through one typed coupling process (CpmCellField), rather than a scripted or lumped stand-in for that coupling?

Findings

  • mechanism A real CPM cell coupled to a real spatio-flux nutrient field through one typed process (CpmCellField) closes the Fig 5 sense/act loop as genuine spatial metabolism: it runs dynamic-FBA at its own footprint and grows both biologically (biomass) and structurally (CPM lattice volume). Confirmed: biomass 0.1 -> ~0.37, CPM volume 32 -> 110 px (~3.4x) over 20 ticks, on a 3600-pixel lattice the cell occupies only ~3% of by the end.
  • mechanism The cell measurably depletes the local nutrient it senses before diffusion partially resupplies it — niche construction as a direct spatial-metabolic consequence, not a scripted trajectory. Confirmed: local footprint glucose concentration peaks at 1.15 (t=2) and falls to 0.94 by t=20, an up-to-~18% local drawdown, while the field-wide glucose total falls by only ~32 of 5940 units — the depletion signature is sharply local to the cell's own footprint and its immediate diffusive neighborhood, not a field-wide event.
  • mechanism The O2-capped dynamic-FBA mechanism forces the optimizer into mixed-acid acetate overflow (rather than pure aerobic respiration), and that byproduct genuinely diffuses outward into a spreading plume via spatio-flux's own DiffusionAdvection process — the secretion side of the coupling is as real as the uptake side. Confirmed: field-wide acetate 0 -> ~47.0 over 20 ticks.

Cell–Cell Coupling, Spatial Accepted

Composing N real CPM cells with one shared, real spatio-flux nutrient field through a single N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduces Fig 3's viability-negotiation pattern as genuine spatial multicellularity, not a scripted or lumped stand-in for it: over 20-tick runs, an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4) drives spatial competitive exclusion (biomass 237.9 vs 64.5, a 3.69x margin; volume 3511 vs 81 px), while a different composite over the same coupling process — a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming role — sustains a cell that never touches glucose (local glucose 0.0) purely via a diffusing cross-feeding handoff (local acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79). Both regimes run through the same process; only the composite's cell roles and field constants differ, mirroring draft-to-living-cell/cell-cell-coupling's law-4 pattern (two handler environments negotiate the shared-resource constraint differently over one interface) at real spatial, per-footprint metabolic resolution.

Question & findings

Question

Does the Fig 3 viability-negotiation pattern hold as real spatial multicellularity — multiple CPM cells sharing one diffusing nutrient field, each running its own dynamic-FBA step at its own footprint, producing both competitive exclusion and cross-feeding — when the whole colony is composed from independent frameworks through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField), rather than the lumped two-role, shared-scalar-pool stand-in draft-to-living-cell's Fig 3 study used?

Findings

  • mechanism Multiple real CPM cells sharing one real spatio-flux nutrient field through one N-cell coupling process (CpmColonyField) reproduce Fig 3's competitive-exclusion branch as genuine spatial multicellularity: an uptake-capacity asymmetry alone (glucose_vmax 10 vs 4), with each cell running its own independent dFBA solve at its own footprint on the same depleting field, drives a decisive biomass and lattice-volume split. Confirmed: biomass 237.9 vs 64.5 (3.69x), CPM volume 3511 vs 81 px, over a 20-tick run.
  • mechanism Over the same coupling process, a different composite -- a localized glucose depot, a fast-diffusing acetate byproduct, and an acetate-consuming cell role -- sustains a cell that never touches glucose purely via a spatial metabolic handoff: the consumer's local glucose reads 0.0 the entire run while its biomass still climbs, entirely on diffused acetate. Confirmed: consumer local_glucose 0.0, local_acetate 1.30, biomass 1.25 -> 3.79, ending as a viable 65-pixel CPM cell.
  • mechanism The additive two-writer assumption CpmColonyField's N-cell field update depends on -- that disjoint per-cell footprint deltas sum losslessly into one shared field -- is runtime-verified directly and independently of any dFBA mechanism, retiring what was previously an unverified assumption in the flagship study. Confirmed structurally: two synthetic single-pixel writers on disjoint pixels each apply independently and their combined effect exactly matches the sum of the two, over repeated ticks.

Method & shared caveats

Every figure begins as an inert draft — typed, unit-bearing ports and a behavior contract, no dynamics — and is compiled by installing one conforming mechanism, which preserves the interface exactly (compiler law 2) while giving the figure real dynamics. Readouts below are measured, not asserted: every executable and the whole cell were run and their observables recorded.

Limitations (shared across every study)

  • This investigation is at an early stage: only the flagship study is built. The design spec (docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-21-cellular-interface-multicellular-design.md) lays out a complete 9-study analogy to draft-to-living-cell; this file and the flagship study reflect only the first increment of that plan.
  • Every mechanism here is toy-real: plausible constants, not calibrated to a named organism, medium, or literature target. The dynamics demonstrate the coupling pattern (real spatial sensing, metabolism, growth, secretion, diffusion), not a quantitatively validated cell.
  • Read alongside its specification-stage analogue, draft-to-living-cell/cell-environment-coupling: that study closes the same Fig 5 loop over a lumped 9-cell 1-D grid with a scripted-shape cell; this study closes it over a real 60x60 2D CPM cell with genuine shape and a real dynamic-FBA metabolism. Compare the two for the same interface claim at two very different mechanistic resolutions.
